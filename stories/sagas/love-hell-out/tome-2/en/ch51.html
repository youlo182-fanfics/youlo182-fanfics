Battalion was pacing the room, cradling Stefania against her in an attempt to calm her while her wife, on the phone, spoke rapidly in Italian with a doctor. Stefania was burning up, her inconsolable cries echoing through the room, filling Maya with an anxiety she had never felt so intensely. My God, I feel so helpless about what's happening to you, my angel... I'd give anything to take away your pain, to relieve this fever that's burning you up. Why do I feel so helpless? How do other parents do it?

She had taken a deep breath, trying to control the wave of anguish tightening her chest.

What if one day I get held up at work, don't make it in time for you, Steffy... or Andrea, or Dani? What if I have to stay in the office or on an intervention late at night and I'm not there when you need me?

The thought that she might miss important, even essential, moments in her children's lives suddenly crossed her mind.

Would I make it in time to see Steffy's first lacrosse or baseball game, her first race, her first dream? And Dani, my God, I can't even imagine not being there to escort her before her prom or see her perform at the end of her first internship she'll be doing at the marine mammal rehabilitation center. And Andrea... my little guy, will I be able to be there in time to attend his high school graduation or present his with his first probie badge, as I promised myself I would? What about him?

In the last nine years, everything has changed. Every decision I make, every step I take... it's all for them. I can't believe it. Twenty-five years ago, I lived only for the dream of being a fire chief. And now, that dream, as big as it is, pales in comparison to what I feel for them. I can no longer afford to run into the fire without thinking, to be that Maya of yesteryear, the one who threw herself into the flames without hesitation.

But even if the job of fire chief isn't as risky as the others, it will take all the energy I have left. It demands unwavering devotion, a devotion I can no longer offer in the same way, not like before... Because it's to them, to Carina, to Andrea, to Danielle, to Stefania that my devotion now goes.

She had resumed her walk, her steps slower, while guilt continued to gnaw at her. She had bent down to kiss Stefania's burning forehead, feeling the painful warmth against her lips.

Oh, my little heart, if only I could make you feel better... I'd take all this fever, all this pain, if I could. It's unbearable to see you suffer like this... I wish I could take it all, every tear, every cry, every moment of pain... I love you so, so much.

She had felt a pang in her own heart at Stefania's every cry, every hiccup of pain echoing her own helplessness.

I thought I was strong, invincible. That nothing could touch me. But now... you're proof that I'm far more vulnerable than I've ever been. Because I can't do anything to protect you from everything, to take every pain you might feel.

She bit her lip, glancing anxiously at her wife, hoping that a solution would come soon.
