Two days after Thanksgiving, the excitement had barely subsided when Maya and Carina's house was once again filled with palpable enthusiasm. This time, it wasn't for a quiet, warm evening among adults, but for a joyous cacophony of children ready to celebrate an equally important event: Andrea's birthday.

The little boy had turned 6, and for the occasion, Maya and Carina had prepared a firefighter-themed party, in honor of Andrea's passion for all things rescue and uniform-related. Alessandro, his best friend, as well as four other boys and a little girl, Lily-Rose, and their cousins by heart, had been invited to share the moment with him.

The house had been transformed into a miniature fire station: red plastic helmets awaited each child on a small table, plastic badges had been placed next to the plates, and the garage had been set up to host a playful rescue mission. Red and yellow sheets had been hung to simulate a fire using a smoke machine, creating a dynamic and immersive gaming atmosphere.

The first guests had arrived accompanied by their parents, whom Carina and Maya had greeted with their usual smiles. Some of the parents, especially the dads, had been impressed to meet Maya, whose reputation as battalion chief was well known in the neighborhood.

-"Wow, it's an honor to meet a real firefighter" said one of them, an admiring smile on his lips as he shook Maya's hand.

She had laughed softly, a little embarrassed.

-"Oh, I'm just a mother today", she replied humbly.

As for the children, they were only interested in one thing: diving into the world of firefighting that Andrea loved so much. Once all the guests had arrived, the garage had resounded with laughter and enthusiastic shouts as Maya had given them their instructions for the rescue mission.

"Okay, little firefighters!" she'd launched with her usual energy.

"Your mission, should you accept it, is to put out this huge fire!" She had pointed to the colorful sheets, and the children, armed with water pistols, had howled with joy before launching themselves into the assault on the «fire», under the amused gaze of Carina, who was holding Danielle in her arms.

Alessandro, of course, had been appointed head of the brigade, and he took his role very seriously, organizing his «firefighters» with the gravity of a true professional. Andrea, proud as a rooster, had led the attack, determined to show his friends what a «real firefighter» he was, just like his mom Maya.

The gifts had then been unwrapped in the dining room after the birthday cake, where Andrea had been delighted to discover that he had received an array of fire engines, costumes and walkie-talkies.

-"Thank you! Thank you so much!" he had repeated, his eyes shining with excitement.

Danielle, too, had wanted to participate in her own way, asking Maya if she could be «fire chief», and Maya, amused, had presented her with a white plastic helmet specially decorated for her.

When the party had drawn to a close, Alessandro, not wanting to leave his best friend, had shyly asked Carina:

-"Can I stay at your place tonight?"

Carina had exchanged an amused glance with Maya before nodding.

-"Of course you can stay. We'll just go and tell your parents and if they agree we'll make up a bed for you in Andrea's room." Alessandro lived in one of the neighboring houses.

-"Thank you, Andrea's moms".

After the frenzy of playing and unwrapping presents, calm had finally begun to settle in Maya and Carina's house.

Alessandro's parents had agreed to let him sleep over, and the children had played in the birthday boy's room with Danielle.

At 7 p.m., the five of them ate and then watched a short cartoon - it was a holiday, after all, and they were allowed to stay up later.

The children, tired but happy, had been taken to Andrea's room, where a mattress had been prepared for Alessandro, right next to Andrea's, and even Danielle had had her place among the boys. The walls were decorated with posters of firefighters, and toys were scattered on the floor, creating a warm and welcoming atmosphere.

Once settled in, Danielle, Andrea and Alessandro nibbled on the chocolate cookies Carina had baked earlier in the day. The three children chatted in whispers, listening to the soft music that echoed through the house while Maya and Carina remained in the hallway, discreetly watching the children.

- "Do you think they'll go to sleep soon?" had asked Carina, an amused smile on her lips as she listened to the laughter and whispers.

- "Maybe not just yet," Maya had replied, crossing her arms.

"But it's his birthday, so I'm willing to let the party go on a little longer."

After a few minutes, Maya had decided to join the children. She'd entered the room with a big smile, plaid in hand.

"So, firefighters, ready for a bedtime story?"

Andrea's eyes had lit up, and Alessandro had nodded enthusiastically.

"Yes! Tell us a firefighter story!"

Maya had taken her place on the edge of the bed, settling herself comfortably as Danielle had come to curl up against her. She had begun to tell the story of a brave firefighter who had rescued a cat stuck in a tree, with all the thrilling details that children loved so much. As she told the story, Carina had watched them from the doorway, her heart filled with warmth at the sight of her little family.

When the story had finished, Maya had asked the boys what they would like to do if they became firefighters. The children had shared their dreams of adventure, heroic rescues and new equipment. This made Maya laugh, and she went on to stress the importance of teamwork. And Danielle, not to be left out, shared that she was going to be a firefighter for the whales, making everyone laugh.

-"You know, firefighters always work together, like one big family. It's like here," she had said, pointing to the room. "You're all little firefighters, and you have to take care of each other."

The boys had nodded, captivated by Maya's words. Finally, with increasingly heavy eyes, Andrea had whispered, "Can we have a Mommy Firefighter story tomorrow?" the blonde had smiled at the memory of Andrea's former appellation of her with her "Firefighter Maya".

-"Of course, but for tonight, it's time to sleep" Maya had replied with a smile.

The battalion had risen and tucked Danielle in, then wished the three little rascals good night and left the room, leaving the door ajar. Carina waited for her in the corridor.

-"They're adorable," Carina murmured. "I'm so glad Alessandro could stay."

Maya had smiled, a look full of affection for her companion.

-"Me too. They have so much fun together. And they never leave Dani out."

The two women had crossed the corridor and headed for the kitchen for a glass of sweet white wine after a busy day. They had discussed the highlights of the party and how Andrea had shone throughout the day, enthusiastically thanking her friends.

- "I think this party was a real success," Carina had said, sitting back on the sofa with her glass of wine. "And I'm so grateful to have these moments with my family."

Maya had nodded, an expression of satisfaction on her face.

As they sipped their wine, they'd shared laughter and memories of their own childhood birthdays, strengthening their connection. Time passed unnoticed as they reminisced about days gone by and looked ahead to the future.

After a while, they heard whispers and laughter coming from Andrea's room. Carina exchanged a knowing glance with Maya.

-"Do you think they're trying to stay awake?"

- "I bet they're planning a new fire mission," Maya had laughed, getting up to check on them.

They had decided to leave them a little longer in their little firefighting world, knowing that these moments of shared happiness were precious and that their family was blossoming together.

The days had flown by, but they had not been alike. Each day had been punctuated by work, and sometimes, outside of her normal hours, Carina had devoted time to her case for the branch. She had spent hours on the phone with Gabriella, discussing the strategies and details needed to open the practice in Seattle. In between calls, she had visited potential locations, scrutinizing each space with a discerning eye, imagining the ambience she wanted for her future practice.

Occasionally, she and Maya got together to discuss their wedding plans. They had already drawn up a guest list, concocted invitations in the shape of plane tickets, and reserved the Pink Door, which they had privatized for their big day. However, there were still many decisions to be made. They still had to choose the menu that would delight their guests and finalize the decorations that would make the day unforgettable. Carina had often caught herself dreaming of this day, imagining the laughter, tears of joy and love that would fill the room.

On Christmas Eve, an atmosphere of warmth and joy reigned in Maya and Carina's home. Twinkling lights from the tree illuminated the room, while the delicious smells of gingerbread cookies and hot chocolate filled the air. The children were eager to celebrate, running around the house, their laughter mingling with the festive music playing in the background.

When the doorbell rang, Carina rushed to the door with a light heart. She opened it to find Tim and Kate, smiling, with Charlie, their soon-to-be-two-year-old daughter, wrapped in a soft blanket in her mother's arms. The child's cheeks had turned pink with the cold, but her eyes sparkled with curiosity.

- "My best man!" exclaimed Carina, greeting Tim with a warm embrace.

"I'm so glad you came!"

- "We wouldn't have missed it for the world!" had replied Tim, a sincere smile on his lips. He had cast a knowing glance at Maya, who was watching them with a smile. "Mom had to make her choices, but we made ours."

Kate, having approached, had added: "She can spend Christmas's Eve alone if she likes. We're here to celebrate the holidays as a family."

Maya had taken Charlie in her arms, rocking her gently while whispering tender words. The little girl had opened her eyes wide, fascinated by the Christmas magic surrounding her.

-"Merry Christmas, darling" she whispered, before turning her attention to the parents. The Italian had looked at her fiancée with a hint of nostalgia in her heart; she saw her companion with her own little girl back then, when they were just friends, and couldn't help having a feeling she found hard to put into words.

Carina had taken Kate by the arm, leading her inside. "Come on, everything's ready! I even baked cookies for Santa!"

The children had run ahead, eager to discover the gifts that would soon be enthroned at the foot of the tree, as Tim and Kate entered, filling the house with their warm presence. The holiday spirit was in the air, and for Carina, this was the perfect moment, surrounded by those she loved, even if a shadow loomed with Phyllis's absence.

Once inside, the warmth of the house enveloped all those present. The excited children had already started playing with Tim and Kate's daughter Charlie, their cries of delight echoing through the room. Maya had prepared a large table lined with colorful, appetizing dishes: roast turkey, mashed potatoes, green beans with almonds and a variety of pies for dessert. The comforting smell of the food made the taste buds tingle, and every guest could feel the love and effort put into this festive meal.

- "Everything looks delicious," Tim had said as he sat down at the table, a look of admiration on his face.

"I hope you're all hungry," Carina added with a smile, happy to see her family gathered around her.

Laughter and conversation mingled with the smell of cooking as everyone took their places around the table. Once the plates were served, Carina raised her glass to propose a toast.

"To the family," she had said warmly.

"And to those who are not here with us. May their spirit be present in our hearts."

The others had raised their glasses in an echo of murmurs of agreement, sincere smiles on their lips.

As they began to eat, Carina had decided to share an anecdote to brighten the atmosphere.

"You know, this reminds me of my very first Christmas with you, Tim. It was when I was dating Coop. He'd decided to seduce me by baking, but he'd messed up the cakes so badly they ended up charred!"

Laughter had erupted around the table, and Tim had added: "Yes, I remember that! But that's when he got the idea to make those chocolate-covered nuts to compensate, isn't it?"

- "Exactly!" had continued Carina, with a smile on her face.

"But he had forgotten to remove the shells, which made the tasting a little... complicated. I remember he had a little smirk on his face, thinking it would seduce me. But it was mostly the fact that he made me and my little brother Andrea laugh that really charmed me."

- "Ah, Danny!" Tim had sighed, laughing.

"I'd eaten those nuts to support my brother, but I think it had made everyone laugh!"

- "Yes, and I felt like everyone was hiding their disgust by trying not to hurt his feelings," Carina had added with a wink.

"It was an unforgettable Christmas, even without the cakes."

"It was the start of many adventures together, even though we didn't have children then. I never imagined that a few years later, I'd be here with Maya; our children Danny's, Maya's and mine; and all of you around this table."

- "Yes, but it's those memories that make us a family," Maya had added, shaking Carina's hand under the table, a tender smile on her face.

- "I really wish you could have met my brother," Tim told Kate and the firefighter.

"Well for you Maya it would have been a bit strange given the current situation, but I think under different circumstances you would have adored each other."

-"I keep telling her. But all Maya can see is that they would have been in love with the same woman!"

After dinner, while the kids were happily playing with Charlie, Tim had decided to do something special. He had gone into the study, where they would be sleeping with Kate and Charlie tonight, to put a Santa suit. When he'd returned to the family, his tall figure wrapped in red, his faux-fur beard floating around his face, he'd appeared in the living room.

-"Ho ho ho!" he thundered, his voice echoing joyfully around the room.

The children immediately stopped playing, turning their heads with wide-eyed wonder. Andrea, fascinated, had remained frozen, glued to Carina's legs, while Danielle, a little frightened, had clung to Maya's neck, her little eyes watching Santa with suspicion but also with excitement.

-"Look, kids! It's Santa Claus!" had shouted Kate, breaking into merry laughter.

- "Santa, what are you doing here?" Andrea had asked, a mixture of amazement and admiration in her voice.

- "I've come to bring you presents!" had replied Tim, imitating the legendary character's playful tone. "And do you know what? I hear you've been very good this year!"

The children had begun to stir, eager to know what Santa had brought. Tim had taken a few moments to interact with them, asking what they had asked for for Christmas. The room was filled with laughter and happiness, and Danielle's worries had gradually dissipated as she let herself be carried away by the magic of the evening.

At that moment, time seemed suspended, a perfect moment when joy and love reigned, united around this chosen family and their memories in the making.

The children were so lost in the moment, they hadn't noticed that their Uncle Tim had disappeared to make way for Santa Claus. He then took his leave of the family, and the children were too preoccupied with opening their presents to pay attention to the fact that Tim was back two minutes later.

The colorful packages, stacked in a corner of the living room, seemed to sparkle under the tree lights. Andrea, Danielle and the still-crawling Charlie rushed to the pile of presents, their eyes sparkling with excitement.

-"Look, this one's for me!" Andrea exclaimed, holding up a large package decorated with a golden ribbon.

- "Open it, open it!" encouraged Danielle, eager to see what was inside.

The children began to tear open the paper with shrieks of joy, revealing their treasures one by one. Andrea had discovered a model fire engine, complete with miniature accessories.

-"Oh, look, Mommy! A fire truck! I'm going to be a great firefighter like you!" he had shouted, his eyes shining with admiration as he turned to Maya.

-"That's great, my little prince!" Maya replied proudly, her heart overflowing with love for her son.

Charlie, sitting between Kate's legs, had begun to fidget, growing impatient. She straightened up with a mischievous smile, taking in hand a little bell hanging on the tree. With an energetic gesture, she had waved it enthusiastically, producing a joyful tinkle that had attracted the attention of the whole room.

-"Look! Charlie's playing with the bell! She thinks she's Santa Claus!" laughed Carina, charmed by the innocence of the moment.

Tim, his eyes sparkling, had knelt down in front of her.

-"You're ringing the bell, sweetie! Bravo!" He'd held out his arms, and Charlie, after waving the bell one last time, had rushed over to him, a crystalline laugh escaping her lips.

Tim had caught her, lifting her into the air before wrapping her in a tender embrace.

"That's my girl! She's incredible!" he'd cried, his heart overflowing with pride.

The children's laughter and squeals of delight had filled the room as they continued to open presents, discovering board games, picture books and other toys.

After a while, it was time for the adults to open their presents. Carina had received a lovely gold bracelet from Maya, representing their love and commitment to each other, with a small heart-shaped pendant engraved with the initials of each of their children.

Maya, for her part, had been delighted to discover a beautiful cashmere scarf from Tim, which had reminded her of cold days in Seattle.

-"Thank you, Tim! I love it!" she said, wrapping it around her neck with a warm look on her face.

Kate, for her part, had offered Carina a cookbook on Italian cuisine, hoping they could cook together in the future.

-"You're an incredible chef, I'm sure you'll love this one" she added with a smile.

Tim had also prepared a small symbolic gift: a piece of jewelry in tribute to Danny, a bracelet so that everyone could remember him during this festive period.

-"I'd like it if we could all wear this in his honor," he proposed, a touch of emotion in his voice.

The adults nodded, touched by this gesture, while the children played and enjoyed themselves, completely absorbed by their new toys.

As the evening wore on, a warm atmosphere settled in, filled with laughter, hugs and memories in the making. The spirit of Christmas was in the air, uniting all present in a moment of shared joy and love.

The evening continued in a cozy atmosphere, all gathered on the sofa, wrapped in comforting warmth. Andrea was snuggled up to Maya, his eyes sparkling with fatigue as he struggled to stay awake. Danielle, comfortably ensconced on Carina's lap, had already begun to doze, her little eyes slowly closing.

In Kate's arms, Charlie had fallen peacefully asleep, her delicate face soothed by the warmth of the hot chocolate she had so happily sipped moments earlier. The sweet taste of chocolate still floated in the air, and each sip had warmed their hearts while creating the perfect atmosphere for this moment of sharing.

The glow of the fake fireplace projected on the TV created a soft, soothing glow, while the twinkling lights of the fir tree cast dancing shadows around the room. It was as if time had stood still, suspended in this bubble of family happiness.

Tim, sitting on the edge of the sofa, had decided to tell a Christmas story to add to the magic of the evening. His calm, gentle voice enveloped the room, resonating with tenderness.

"Once upon a time, in a small snowy village, there lived a young boy who dreamed of becoming the best toy maker in the world. Every Christmas, he would watch the children's laughter as they played with the toys he had made..."

He had continued his tale, describing the boy's adventures, his magical workshop filled with elves who helped him create wonders, dolls that danced and trains that whistled. Tim's voice became a gentle whisper, and each word seemed to soothe the still-awake children.

As the story progressed, the little ones' excitement began to fade, their eyes slowly closing. Andrea, with her arms around Maya, had finally fallen asleep, while Danielle drifted off to sleep, her head resting against Carina.

Tim had continued his story, the rhythm of his voice setting the pace in the soothing silence of the room, turning every moment into a precious one. The parents were caught up in the magic of the evening, recognizing how lucky they were to be together, enveloped in the love and warmth of their family.

As Tim told the last lines of his story, he had glanced around at his loved ones, a peaceful smile on his face. The magic of Christmas filled the room, uniting their hearts and brightening their spirits with joyful promise for the future.
