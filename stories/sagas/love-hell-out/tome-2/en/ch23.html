June had arrived, and with it the excitement of Carina and Maya's wedding. Preparations were in full swing, but a shadow lingered in Carina's mind. She felt a mixture of apprehension and hope.

Saturday the sixteenth of June had arrived, marking the fifth anniversary of Danny's funeral. Carina had offered to organize a small gathering in memory of the man she had loved. The Italian's heart sank at the thought that Phyllis might not understand her desire for happiness. It had been so long since she'd been at war with herself, torn between her love for Danny and her new life with Maya. But if Danny had really wanted this, then maybe Phyllis could too.

Maya, Andrea, and Danielle had accompanied her to Phyllis's, who had suggested they do it at her place, where Tim, Kate and their little Charlie would also be present. The atmosphere was one of nostalgia, with everyone sharing stories and memories of Danny.

After a few hours of discussion, laughter and even tears, Phyllis had finally stood up, her gaze on the Italian.

- "Carina," she'd begun, her voice full of emotion, "this day is always difficult, and I've never known how to talk about it with you."

Carina, had looked at her with rapt attention, nodded, aware of unspoken words and past tensions.

"I know it's been a long time since I received your invitation to the wedding, and I've never really answered you," Phyllis had continued. Time had seemed to freeze as Carina absorbed the request. Her thoughts whirled. What if it went wrong? But deep down, a glimmer of hope shone through. She wanted to believe that their relationship could be rebuilt, that her family could grow, even if it meant facing painful memories.

"I know it's hard for me to accept your relationship with Maya," Phyllis continued. "But... I remembered what Danny would have wanted. He would have wanted you to be happy, to rebuild your life after his loss... I thought about it, and I realized that I don't want to be the one who misses important moments in my grandchildren's lives."

The revelation had made Carina's heart flutter. Phyllis's words resonated within her, warming her heart. She had never dared to hope that her mother-in-law could give her this blessing. It didn't mean the wounds were healed, but it was a first step towards an acceptance that seemed so far away. She had felt Phyllis's anguish slowly dissipate, and a dawning hope was growing within her.

- "So... can I ask you, if it's not too late to come to your wedding, June twenty-third,... I know it's a week away..."

Carina's eyes had flashed with emotion. She had taken a deep breath before answering, a wave of emotion washing over her.

- "Do you really want to come?" she'd asked, her voice tight with emotion.

- "Yes," Phyllis had replied with a slight smile. "I want to see my daughter get married, even if it's not to my Danny. Maya... she's not such a bad choice. I can see how much she loves children and you. I can tell."

Carina had felt an immeasurable warmth in her heart at those words. Shaking Phyllis's hand, Carina had felt light, as if a huge weight had been lifted from her shoulders. For the first time in a long time, she could imagine a future where everyone could coexist, where love could triumph over pain. It would still be a long road ahead, but now she was certain it would be less lonely. She had taken Phyllis's hand, squeezing it gratefully. She knew how much Phyllis's words could weigh, but she wondered if this conversation might finally pave the way to mutual understanding. Was it possible that the love she had for Maya could be accepted?

- "It would mean a lot to us, Mrs. Cooper. I'd really like you to be there. Thank you so much."

Phyllis had sketched a smile, a mixture of relief and happiness.

- "I'm glad to hear that." Then she'd added with a note of gentleness in her voice: "And maybe it's time you called me by my first name, now. I'm ready to make an effort for this new family."

Carina had smiled, her heart overflowing with gratitude and affection.

- "Okay, Phyllis," she'd replied, the word slipping out with joyful lightness.

Maya, who had watched the scene with a wry smile, had felt immense satisfaction as she saw the barriers collapse between the two women.

After this emotional exchange, a lighter atmosphere began to settle over the room. The children's laughter echoed in the air, filled with joy and complicity. Andrea and Danielle bickered happily around Maya, sharing memories of their games and adventures together.

Tim, seated on the sofa, had observed the scene with a satisfied smile.

-"You know, Maya," he had said, looking at the complicity between her and the children, "it's clear they adore you. It's like they've always wanted you to be part of their lives."

Maya had smiled, feeling touched by these words.

-"They're incredible. Every day with them is a gift." Her gaze had fallen on Andrea and Danielle, who had exchanged a knowing glance before returning to their games.

Phyllis, observing the scene with a tender smile, had decided to enter the conversation.

- "So, Maya," she had begun, addressing her directly with a friendly note, "are you ready for the big day, which is only a week away?"

Maya had taken a deep breath, sensing excitement mixed with slight nervousness.

-"I think I'm almost there. There are still a few details to finalize, but I'm really looking forward to this moment."

- "And have you thought about how you're going to involve the children during the ceremony?" had asked Phyllis, curiously. "I imagine they'll want to play an important role."

Carina had exchanged a knowing glance with Maya.

-"Oh, we've planned some entertainment for them during the adult meal. And Danielle and Andrea will be bringing our wedding rings, which I'm sure they'll love."

Phyllis had smiled, her eyes lighting up at the thought of the children playing such a special role. "It's such an excellent idea. They'll be so proud to have such an important task to do."

Maya had felt an inner warmth, pleased to see Phyllis's enthusiasm.

- "Yes, and I think it will help them feel even more involved in the day."

At that moment, the children's laughter had mingled with the memories evoked, and a feeling of warmth had spread through the room. Maya had realized how good she felt, accepted among this family, despite the difficult past.

- "You know," Maya had resumed, with a slight smile, "I'll try not to stress too much. After all, it's a celebration, isn't it?"

- "Absolutely," Tim had replied, shaking his head. "And above all, don't forget that it's not perfection that counts, but the love that unites you."

Maya had smiled, comforted by Tim's words. She had felt a surge of gratitude towards this family who, though imperfect, were ready to welcome her.

Right now, I can't stop smiling. I never thought I could feel so at home here, in this house where Danny had grown up, surrounded by his family. Sitting in this living room, I feel a sense of peace, as if, despite the complicated past and the pain, I finally belong. Phyllis, though loaded with complex emotions, seems willing to take a step towards me, and every exchange, every look she gives me gives me hope that, perhaps, we could build something beautiful together.

My eyes land on Carina. In a week's time, I'll be marrying her, at last. This long-awaited day is within reach, a dream come true after five long years of waiting. My heart races at the thought of having her by my side, of pledging my love to her in front of everyone who matters to us. I can't stop repeating to myself how much I love her, how beautiful and perfect she is for me. This woman, with her gentleness and strength, gives meaning to my life, and I know in my heart that no other love could ever compare to the one I feel for her.

As I look at her, a radiant smile lighting up her face, I realize how essential she has become to my life. I never thought I could love someone so deeply. It makes me think of Andrea and Danielle. Seeing them playing together, laughing out loud, fills my heart with an indescribable warmth. I never thought I could love such small human beings, but they found their way into my heart with such ease. Every glance exchanged, every moment shared strengthens the special bond I'm beginning to forge with them.

Perhaps this day, this family reunion, marks the beginning of a new story, one full of laughter and shared happiness. A chapter where fears and doubts would fade away to make way for love and complicity. Seeing Phyllis interact with my children, feeling Tim and Kate's support, I tell myself that this family I'm discovering is more open than it has ever been. It's like a breath of fresh air, a promise of what could be.

I stand for a moment, melting into the children's laughter, realizing that I've never wanted anything but this: a family. I've created one, and while the road hasn't been easy, it's been worth it. My heart overflows with gratitude and happiness. I know I'm ready to embrace this new life, surrounded by the people I love.

Looking at her, Carina realized that Maya had found her place among them, that the walls that had separated them were beginning to crumble. She had approached Maya and, while gently caressing her lover's hand, had whispered:

-"I'm so happy you're here with us."

Maya had inclined her head, her eyes shining with emotion.

-"And so am I. It's everything I've ever dreamed of."

The evening had continued with exchanges of memories, laughter and promises of happiness to come. Each of these conversations, each of these laughs, cemented a little more the new family that had formed around Carina and Maya.

Carina, Gabriella, Jo, Brooke and Hailey had met a few weeks after Josephine and Carina's glimpse of each other, after the five-year anniversary of Danny's death, a few days before the big day, in front of the first building of their marathon day. It was a modest building in a quiet residential area of Capitol Hill, surrounded by trees, with a small parking space at the back. Carina was the first to get out of the car, followed closely by Gabriella and Jo.

- "Okay, this is the first one..." announced Hailey, a map in hand. "We've got about forty-five minutes before our next appointment."

- "Let's check it out," said Gabriella, clearly concentrating.

They'd walked through the front door to discover a bright, but somewhat partitioned space, with rooms smaller than they'd expected. Brooke had glanced around critically.

- "It's... cosy," she said, crossing her arms.

Jo had taken a full tour of the main room, observing the windows and room layout.

-"Yes, but it seems a bit limited in terms of space. We could fit in one or two cabinets, but not much more... And our ambitions are bigger."

Carina had nodded, sensing a hint of pride in Dr. Wilson for fitting in so well and using the word «our».

-"I agree. It's pretty, but we may quickly become cramped if we want to grow or accommodate several practitioners."

Gabriella had turned to Hailey, who was carefully reading the details of the lease.

- "What about operating costs?"

- "It's one of the cheapest I've found, but we'd also have to count on major renovations to make it a modern practice," Hailey explained. "We could transform it, but that requires work, and I'm not sure the investment is worth it in the long run."

Brooke had frowned.

- "If we have to plan work already, we lose the price advantage."

Carina had smiled at Brooke, amused by her ever-honed practicality.

-"That's true. Well, let's move on to the next one, then."

They had exited the building and headed for their next visit.

The second site was downtown, in a more modern building amidst skyscrapers. The elevator had taken them to the fifth floor, where they'd discovered a large open room with an unobstructed view of the Seattle skyline.

- "It's impressive," Jo murmured, approaching the windows.

"But it also gives the impression of a somewhat impersonal space, doesn't it?"

Gabriella had nodded.

-"I know what you mean. We could make it a beautiful place, but it's perhaps too corporate for a clinic that aims to be warm and welcoming."

- "And then there's the parking issue," Hailey added.

"The rent is high, and patients might find it complicated to get here. Not to mention the traffic noise might get in the way."

Brooke sighed.

- "That's true, and the access isn't ideal for patients who would need quiet and comfort, especially those who will be coming for post-delivery care."

Carina had looked around thoughtfully.

- "It's beautiful, but I don't feel... connected to this place."

- "Me neither." confirmed Jo.

- "Then out." Gabriella concluded, closing her notebook.

The last visit of the day had taken them to South Lake Union. As soon as they approached the building, they sensed something different. The place was spacious, located right next to the lake, in a neighborhood that was both dynamic and rapidly developing, surrounded by modern buildings, startups and trendy restaurants. The building itself was elegant, with a glass façade and a large, bright entrance.

-"I think we're going to like this one". Hailey said with a confident smile.

They entered, greeted by a vast, airy hall. The ceilings were high, and every room seemed bathed in natural light. The rooms were large enough to accommodate several practices, and there was even a space that could be set up as a warm, comfortable waiting room. Jo was the first to speak.

-"Wow. This one... it's got potential."

Carina smiled broadly as she looked around.

-"Yes, I feel it too. There's so much room to grow."

- "We could easily set up four or five practices here, with enough space for each practitioner," Brooke had mused as she inspected one of the adjacent rooms. "And there's even room for an additional treatment room or space for specialized consultations."

- "and in the back room we could make a laboratory!" Carina added.

-"And there's no problem with parking," said Hailey. "There's plenty of parking right next door. Not to mention that the neighborhood is booming, so we could attract a varied clientele."

Gabriella, who had so far remained silent, had taken a look around one of the main rooms before returning to the group.

-"The space is perfect. We can plan here for the long term."

- "And the place is accessible for patients, close to several hospitals, which is a huge asset." Jo clarified.

- "What's more," Carina continued, "the neighborhood is lively, with lots of young families. It would be a good area to attract patients at different stages of their lives."

- "I think we've got our winner," Brooke said, a smile in her voice.

- "I agree," affirmed the Italian. "This is where our practice should be born!"

Hailey had nodded, delighted.

-"I'll take care of the paperwork for this building right away, then. We've got to act fast before it passes us by."

- "We'll make sure that doesn't happen," Carina replied with determination. "This is the one."

All had nodded in agreement, sensing that they were taking a decisive step towards their future project. As they exited the building, Carina had turned her gaze to Jo.

"I think this place is going to bring us luck."

Jo had smiled: "Me too. I can't wait to see what we do with it."

As well as being perfect in itself for Carina and Jo's activities the building had another advantage, it wasn't far from where she and Maya lived.

A few days later, in an elegant meeting room bathed in the natural light of a balmy Seattle afternoon,
Carina, Gabriella, Jo, Brooke, Hailey, Anaïs and Sarah were seated around a large table in the bright meeting room of the temporary office. Computers and tablets were ready for the videoconference with the New York investors. The atmosphere was studious, but also palpably excited. Carina, always enthusiastic, exchanged knowing glances with Jo, while Gabriella, impeccably dressed as usual, checked the final details of the presentation.

- "Looks like everything's ready," said Hailey, adjusting her microphone.

- "Perfect," nodded Sarah, the group's lawyer, as she opened her file.

"I'll also take advantage of this call to give you an update on the status of the paperwork for the sale and lease."

Carina had nodded gratefully. Sarah had been an indispensable asset for all the legal management, whether for the firm or the real estate dealings with Hailey.

The videoconference had started, and the familiar faces of the New York investors had appeared on the screen: David Castillo, serious as ever, Thomas Harper, a little more relaxed, and the Pearson couple, smiling and warm.

-"Hello everyone!" Carina exclaimed enthusiastically, happy to see the whole team together, even from a distance.

- "Hello!" replied David, adjusting his camera. "I hope everything's going well in Seattle. I imagine you have some interesting news to share."

- "Absolutely," replied Gabriella, taking over. "First of all, we'd like to formally introduce you to Josephine Wilson, our future collaborator in Seattle. Jo, I'll let you introduce yourself."

Josephine had smiled and spoke confidently.

-"Hello everyone, I'm Dr. Josephine Wilson, OB-GYN at Grey Sloan Memorial here in Seattle. I'm thrilled to be joining this dynamic team and helping to open this practice. It's a project I'm really passionate about, and I look forward to working with all of you."

- "Nice to meet you, Dr. Wilson," Thomas grinned. "We've heard great things about you from Dr. DeLuca and Dr. Aurora."

Catherine Pearson had added jovially, "It's great to see the team growing with such promising talent. Welcome aboard!"

- "Thank you very much," Jo replied, touched.

Carina had spoken again, excited by what was to come.

-"We also have some excellent news about the premises. After visiting three locations with Jo, Brooke, Hailey and Gabriella, we all agreed on the one that would be perfect for our Seattle practice."

The faces on the screen seemed to come alive at this mention.

- "And what is your choice?" asked David.

- "We decided on the building near South Lake Union," Brooke explained. "The location is ideal for attracting a diverse clientele, close to major hospitals yet in a booming neighborhood."

Hailey had nodded.

- "And there's good development potential for long-term partnerships, which is crucial to our expansion vision."

Gabriella had spoken up to complete, "This choice wasn't just strategic. The location offers a welcoming, modern atmosphere, perfect for our patients. We want this practice to be not only a place of care, but also a space where women will feel supported throughout their journey. And I'm sure we'll manage to find a similar location in New York!"

- "Speaking of which," Sarah had interjected, "I've already started the formalities for the lease. Everything should be finalized in the next few weeks. Hailey and I are also working out the details regarding the acquisition of furniture and medical equipment."

Robert Pearson had nodded, visibly pleased.

-"It looks well advanced. You've thought of everything."

Carina had smiled, delighted that the investors were so receptive.

-"We're doing our best. There are still a few adjustments to be made, but we're well on the way. The opening is still about a year away. Between city approval, all the paperwork, setting up the various practices and various hires."

- "And in terms of financing and projections?" Thomas had asked, still concerned about the financial aspect.

Gabriella had replied calmly.

- "We're well on schedule, with a slightly lower budget than we'd estimated. This gives us a little leeway in case of unforeseen circumstances. What's more, Carina and her future wife have already signed up for fund-raising events."

-"Excellent," said David, satisfied. "We have every confidence in you. It looks like Seattle is in very good hands."

- "Thank you, David," Carina replied with a radiant smile. "We're all very motivated by this project, and with Doctor Wilson on board, we have a strong team to carry out this vision."

- "We can't wait to see how it all develops," Catherine Pearson concluded with a warm smile. "Good luck to you all."

After a few friendly closing exchanges, the videoconference came to an end. The room fell back into a contented silence, broken by the sound of chairs being pushed back.

-"It went well," Hailey said, rising to her feet.

- "Yes, really," Gabriella had agreed, turning her head towards Carina and Jo. "You were perfect. Jo, welcome officially to the team."

Jo had smiled, a gleam of pride in her eyes. "Thank you, I'm ready to give it my all."

- "and now... Ready to party. Our only concern for the next seventy-two hours must be the wedding of our favorite obstetrician!" Gabriella said, coming over to give her long-time friend a friendly shake on the shoulders.
