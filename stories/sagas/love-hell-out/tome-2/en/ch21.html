On Friday morning, Carina and Maya had a hearty breakfast at Café Mogador, a charming Moroccan café with a warm atmosphere, famous for its poached eggs and pita bread. With the energy of coffee and croissants, they had left the café, eager to start their day.

They had decided to revisit Central Park, taking in the picturesque paths and green spaces. Hand in hand, they wandered around the lake, admiring the boats and joggers, and reminiscing about their first visit. They even shared a romantic moment on the Bow Bridge, promising each other to return here on every trip.

After a leisurely morning, they headed for the Metropolitan Museum of Art, where the immensity of the works of art had plunged them into a world of wonder. They explored the halls, spending a long time in front of the famous paintings by Van Gogh and Monet, sharing their thoughts on each piece.

In the evening, they headed to the J.G. Melon restaurant, renowned for its juicy burgers, where they tasted the best burger in New York. Accompanied by crispy fries and a craft beer, dinner had become a convivial moment, enhanced by laughter and gentle glances.

To conclude their second day, Carina and Maya attended the Broadway musical Dirty Dancing. They were captivated by the performance and the chemistry between the actors, recalling their own dance memories while sharing smiles and chills at every scene.

On Saturday morning, after a light breakfast at a local bakery, they took the ferry to the Statue of Liberty. As they approached the island, Carina had taken Maya's hand, both women admiring the majesty of the statue that symbolized hope and freedom. They enjoyed the view over Manhattan, taking photos of each other from every angle.

Back on the island of Manhattan, they strolled down Wall Street, past the famous bull and impressive buildings. Maya, fascinated by the city's energy, had taken a photo in front of the New York Stock Exchange, her smile beaming with satisfaction.

For lunch, they had stopped at Los Tacos No.1 in Chelsea Market, enjoying delicious tacos with fresh salsa. After lunch, they decided to head to the High Line, a suspended urban park, where they enjoyed the urban landscapes, sculptures and nature in the heart of the city.

In the evening, they booked a table at L'Artusi, a chic Italian restaurant, where they enjoyed refined dishes in a romantic atmosphere. It was the perfect place to share stories and laughter over a glass of wine, before taking a cab to the Empire State Building and climbing to the top to admire the city lights and experience a romantic « Sleepless Nights » moment.

On Sunday morning, after breakfast at the hotel, they decided to visit the Museum of Modern Art (MoMA), exploring intriguing contemporary works and installations. Carina, interested in art, had taken the time to explain some of the works to Maya, sharing her vision and enthusiasm, before heading back down Fifth Avenue, which they had already walked a little after the MET, and admiring the luxury boutiques, while heading towards Rockefeller Center and climbing to the very top of the Top Of The Rock to take some impressive photos of New York by day, in contrast to those at the top of the Empire State Building, which had been at night.

In the afternoon, they headed for the SoHo district, where they shopped in unique boutiques, allowing themselves to splurge on clothes and souvenirs from their escapade, both for themselves and for the children. Maya had even had Carina try on a pretty dress she'd given her, wrapping her in a sweet kiss before buying it.

For dinner, they had chosen The River Café, located under the Brooklyn Bridge, offering a breathtaking view of the Manhattan skyline. The romantic setting, with twinkling lights, had made the meal unforgettable. They ended their evening with a stroll on the Brooklyn Bridge, savoring the cool breeze while discussing their dreams for the future, before returning to Time Square to enjoy the night-time ambience.

On Monday morning, they awoke with smiles on their faces, enjoying a final breakfast at Balthazar, an iconic French café in SoHo, famous for its viennoiseries and Parisian ambience.

Before leaving the city, they had decided to take one last trip to Times Square to buy tourist souvenirs for their loved ones. Amazed by the lights and the energy of the place, they took photos of themselves in the middle of the crowd, immortalizing their trip one last time.

Finally, they took a cab back to the airport, their hearts filled with precious memories. The promise of returning to New York, even with the children, to share many more adventures and explore the wonders of the world together floated in the air.

In the cab, hand in hand, they had exchanged knowing glances, aware that this escapade was much more than just a trip: it was a chapter in their story, a celebration of their love and their desire to explore the world together.

On the plane from New York to Seattle, Carina and Maya were comfortably seated in business class. The spacious seats and impeccable service provided an atmosphere conducive to relaxation. As the plane began to leave the tarmac, Carina couldn't help but feel a surge of excitement. She leaned towards Maya, a mischievous smile on her lips.

-"You know, I was wondering..." she began, her playful tone hinting at a daring idea. "How about a little intimate time in the airplane lavatory?"

Maya had smiled, blushing slightly, Carina really was a woman full of surprises, but a gleam of mischief had crossed her eyes. She had remembered a previous exchange where Carina had expressed her disinterest in such places, in making love in public toilets. So, with an air of seriousness, she had taken a formal tone and begun to address her companion in formalities.

-"Hello, I couldn't help noticing that you have several stamps on your passport. Far be it from me to be intrusive, but... do you travel a lot?"

Carina, surprised but amused, couldn't help but flash a huge grin.

- "Here, bambina?"

-"Excuse me, do we know each other? I'd remember if I'd ever met such a beautiful woman."

Carina had straightened up, feigning dignity.

-"Indeed, I travel a lot. I'm... an international development consultant."

- "Wow, impressive!" replied Maya, clearly interested.

"You must live with your head in the clouds."

- "I love to travel, believe me, but sometimes I wish I had someone waiting for me back home... A pretty woman who would make me want to come home more to Seattle... where I live."

Maya had tilted her head slightly.

- "Oh?! Seattle! I live right there..."

- "And what do you do in Seattle?" asked Carina, intrigued.

-"I'm an architect."

-"Architect?! And after that, you think I'm impressive?!" exclaimed Carina with a wink.

"And why were you in New York?" continued the Italian, full of spirit.

-"I designed a house for one of my clients in Seattle, and she was so impressed with my work that she asked me to come to New York to design another house for her. She's bought a place that she wants to partly raze or extend, and she asked me to come and see the place for real to get a feel for it."

-"What was I saying? Impressive..." Said the brunette with a smirk and a look of intense brown.

"I'm sorry, I haven't introduced myself, I'm... Giulia"

-"Oh!" said a surprised Maya. "Nice to meet you... Giulia, I'm... Jordan"

- "It's me who's delighted, Jordan"

Their exchanges were punctuated by laughter and knowing glances. The stewardess, in passing, couldn't help but smile at the palpable dynamic between the two women.

-"What can I get you to start with?" she asked, pausing for a moment to admire their complicity.

-"A glass of white wine for me, please" replied Carina, before turning to Maya.

"And you, what are you having?"

- "I'll have a bourbon, thank you!" Maya replied, her eyes sparkling.

As the hostess walked away, Carina had leaned a little closer to Maya.

-"You know, I've always wanted to see how an architect works. I imagine you must have some incredible ideas."

«Jordan», flattered, began to talk about her projects, passionate about her profession. The two women had discovered what they had in common: a passion for creation, art, and the desire to build something beautiful, be it a building or a relationship.

- "It's fascinating" murmured the Italian, her eyes shining.

"I never thought I'd meet such an inspiring woman on an airplane."

- "And I never thought this flight would be so captivating. I could stand here talking to you for hours..." replied Maya, a charming smile on her lips.

-"Perhaps we could... Be on first-name terms, Jordan?"

- "With pleasure Giulia... With pleasure."

The drinks had arrived, and the two women toasted, their glasses gently clinking together in a crystalline noise.

- "To new encounters and adventures to come," Carina proposed.

- "To journeys together, perhaps?" added Maya, a twinkle in her eye.

As the flight progressed, they had continued to flirt, each revealing a little more about themselves. «Giulia» had explained how she often found herself on the move, juggling between different countries to help develop humanitarian projects. Maya had shared anecdotes of her travels, her architectural projects around the world, all the while captivating Carina with her tales.

As the plane passed through the clouds, they had continued their exchanges, laughing and discovering each other, immersing themselves in this magical moment of flirtation and connection, while planning possible future escapades together. The smell of coffee and champagne wafted through the air, but for them, the real elixir was the promise of a shared future filled with discovery and love.

As the plane began its descent into Seattle, «Giulia» boldly began tracing little circles on the back of «Jordan's» hand on the armrest between them.

Maya felt a shiver run down her arm, her breath catching for a moment at the unexpected contact. The little circles Carina traced were gentle, but they awakened in her a subtle warmth, a desire to prolong this intimate moment. Her heart quickened slightly as she found herself wishing that this flight would never end. Carina, for her part, savored this newfound closeness, feeling a pleasant tension in the silence that had settled between them. She knew that something greater, something deeper, was being born at this moment.

-"I don't want to get too far ahead of myself, but... Maybe we could meet again... In Seattle..."

- "and maybe now you'll have a good reason to come back to Seattle after your travels..."

- "I think I've found a very good reason to come home..."

Landing in Seattle, after a flight full of innuendo and exchanged smiles, «Giulia» and «Jordan» had walked down the long corridor to the terminal. The atmosphere between them was light, but tinged with a palpable tension, as if every moment spent together brought them a little closer.

Maya had stopped just after reaching the exit, in front of the disembarkation gates, turning to face Carina. Her gaze lingered on the Italian's features, hesitating for a moment before pronouncing, with a smirk on her lips:

-"I'm delighted to have made your acquaintance. You have my phone number now... The ball's in your court, Giulia."

Carina had let a smile stretch her lips before approaching Maya, gently, with that natural assurance that characterized her. She had placed her delicate hand on Maya's cheek, caressing her skin with her fingertips, her thumb tracing a tender path over her cheekbone. Then, without warning, she'd leaned in to place an intense but chaste kiss on her lips. A kiss that had lasted long enough to let them savor the moment, without crossing the border of passion. When she had pulled back slightly, she had whispered against Maya's mouth, her eyes still closed, their breaths still mingled:

-"I'm sorry, I wanted to the whole time... Can we consider the ball in your court now, Jordan?"

The scene, seen from the outside, would have seemed straight out of a romantic movie. Two strangers who had just met on a flight, exchanging a passionate farewell in an airport after falling in love at first sight. Or perhaps lovers parting on a station platform or at a departure gate. There was something timeless, a magic that enveloped them.

Maya, or rather «Jordan» in this improvised staging, had smiled against Carina's lips, her voice barely a whisper, full of hope and promise:

- "Giulia..." she'd begun softly, savoring the name as if it belonged to a story they were about to write together. Then, in a breath imbued with reality:
"Now what do you say we go get our kids?"

Carina had burst out laughing, stepping back with amusement, nudging her fiancée's shoulder.

- "You just broke everything, Maya Bishop!"

Maya had shrugged with a mischievous smile.

- "Jordan and Giulia can always decide to see each other again... But Maya's missing her babies."

Carina's gaze had instantly softened, a loving glint in her eyes. She had taken her fiancée's hand, intertwining their fingers tenderly before leading her to the luggage conveyor. They retrieved their suitcases and headed for the parking lot to find their car. Knowing they lived north of Seattle, they'd have to head south first to Tim and Kate's house, about an hour's drive away, to pick up their children. The drive would be a bit long, but the excitement of seeing Danielle and Andrea again made every mile lighter.

In the car, as Maya drove south on the highway, Carina had turned to her, a mischievous smile stretching her lips.

-*Thank you for agreeing to share this cab with me... Jordan..." she had breathed, mischievously.

Maya had smiled back, playing the game with ease. They'd been flirting the whole flight, so why stop now?

- "With pleasure, Giulia," she replied, her gaze focused on the road, but an amused twinkle in her eyes.

"I had no idea we lived so close together."

Their complicity, born of simple role-playing, had endured, even beyond their flight. Their light, teasing exchanges filled the cabin of the car, a way for them to prolong a shared moment by letting an air of newness float between them, despite the years they'd spent together.

After an hour's drive, they arrived at Tim and Kate's house. The reunion with Danielle and Andrea had been a warm one, with the children running to their mothers with cries of joy. With their arms full of hugs and laughter, Maya and Carina felt that their family had finally been reunited. They presented Tim, Kate and Charlie with gifts to thank them for looking after their babies.

On the way back to north Seattle, where they lived, the atmosphere in the car was filled with happiness. The children enthusiastically recounted their weekend with Tim and Kate, while Maya and Carina exchanged knowing glances.

As the familiar silhouette of their house finally appeared, they knew that while their game between «Jordan» and «Giulia» was a nice escape, it was Maya and Carina, surrounded by their children, who had built what really mattered.

It was a sunny Friday, May the twenty-fifth, exactly five years after their lives had been turned upside down. Five years since Maya had fallen in love with Carina at first sight, and five years since the Italian had lost Danny, her partner and the father of her children, in tragic circumstances. On each anniversary of his death, they came here, to his grave, to lay flowers with Andrea and Danielle, perpetuating a tradition that bound them all to his memory.

Maya and Carina walked silently hand in hand, the children running a little ahead of them with their little bouquets of flowers clutched in their hands. Six-year-old Andrea understood better than ever what this visit meant. As for Danielle, she kept holding on to the flowers she wanted to lay «for Dad», trying to keep up with her brother. They reached the grave where Danny's name was engraved, sober but powerful.

Carina knelt first, her fingers brushing the cold stone, a slight smile tinged with sadness on her lips. Maya had remained a little behind, respecting this moment that had belonged to Carina for so many years. She still remembered the day she had met Carina, and how her heart had been broken by the sight of this strong, distraught woman to whom she had just announced the death of her partner, the father of her children. Now, five years later, this suffering had become part of their shared life, an indelible bond that brought them closer together.

The children sat on the grass, right in front of their father's grave, with the spontaneity befitting their age. They didn't understand all the pain behind that stone, but they felt how important it was to come and talk to him.

Andrea was the first to speak:

-"Dad, it's us. For Easter we went on an egg hunt at Grandma and Uncle Tim's... and we found lots of chocolate!" He'd smiled as he remembered the moment.

"Charlie found eggs too, she's still too little to eat as many as we did, but she's so cute!"

Danielle, who was only four, had taken a deep breath, determined to join in.

- "Well, Daddy, I played with Charlie, and then we made a cake with Grandma, you know she's your mommy, and Auntie Kate, she's Uncle Tim's lover, you know he's your brother. It was c'ocolate and vanilla, but you know I don't really like vanilla," she added in a serious tone, as if it were a very important fact.

Andrea had continued with that sincerity that belongs only to children:

-"Daddy, I want to be a fireman like you and Mommy! Alessandro is my best friend, he wants to be a fireman too, and we're going to work together! Just like Mum did when she worked with her best friend, Auntie Andy."

Danielle had nodded enthusiastically, her eyes shining.

-"I want to be a whale vet! And turtles, and orcas too!" She counted on her little fingers.

"And Z'ulie and Élodie, z'ey're coming to stay with us this weekend... I mean tomorrow! I to'd them we were going to see dolphins one day."

Maya and Carina, still in the background, held hands. Their eyes met, knowingly, as they listened to the children share their little worlds with Danny. They knew that these moments were essential for the children to have a link, however imaginary, with their father. It was also why they talked about him so often, or why Maya always told stories about firefighters that included him to put them to sleep at night.

When the children had finished talking, Maya gently approached the grave. She knelt beside Carina and gently caressed the stone before whispering, her voice filled with emotion:

- "Thank you, Lieutenant Cooper... Thank you for letting me into this family. I take care of them, I hope you're okay with that, wherever you are. I hope you're happy that your family continues to move forward, with someone who cherishes them and watches over them as if they were her own, who loves them as you would have loved them... and I hope you know that you can rest in peace."

Carina had closed her eyes for a moment, her hand still resting on the grave. She had taken a deep breath before speaking in turn, her voice soft but full of intensity:

-"I'll always love you, Danny. Nothing will ever change that." She had smiled as she looked at Maya, then at the children who were now playing with some pebbles near the grave.

"But I also know that it was you who put her in my path. I never doubted it. And for that, I'll thank you every day. You gave me these two wonderful children, and you continue to guide us."

The silence that followed was soothing. There was something beautiful in that moment, something that belonged only to them.

Carina had placed a final kiss on her fingers before placing them on the stone, before straightening up with Maya. They had taken the children's hands, and together, had begun to walk away, leaving behind colorful flowers that would remind Danny that, even after five years, he still remained in their hearts.

Walking back to the car, Maya had slipped her fingers between Carina's and given her a smile filled with love and gratitude.

- "Five years..." she murmured. "Five years since I met you..."

The Italian had smiled back, with tears in her eyes, but tears of gratitude and love.

- "And five years in which, thanks to you, I've never been alone."
