Once again, I have the honor of writing the afterword for this new story, which transported me from the very beginning. It is beautifully written, and all the emotions came flooding in from the first few chapters.

I became genuinely attached to all the characters and to this family. This is not just Marina's story, but a story about love and family ties. I was able to read about how their story unfolded and how they faced trials with resilience. Maya was the light in Carina's and the children's lives, and thanks to her unwavering love, they were able to find joy and love again. She became the pillar of this family, and Steffy came to strengthen its foundations.

I loved seeing the relationships between the characters evolve. Maya and Carina were close and supportive of each other. But I also loved Maya's relationship with the children and how they became her whole life. I really loved the relationship between Maya and Andrea and the unique bond they shared.

This story is a beautiful lesson that teaches us that life can be beautiful even when we think we'll never recover from a difficult experience. We can also achieve all our dreams, and it's easier when we're surrounded by and supported by the people we love. This love and this family are inspiring, and everyone deserves to experience that.

Thank you to Julie for her trust and for allowing me to escape from everyday life once again. Thank you for all these emotions.

"Being a family means you're part of something wonderful. It means you'll love and be loved for the rest of your life." (Lisa Weed)

Melany Routier
