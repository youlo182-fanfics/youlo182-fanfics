/Maya's Bachelorette Party /

Maya's bachelorette party was held in a trendy downtown bar, decorated with string lights and pastel balloons. Laughter echoed in the air, creating a joyous, festive atmosphere. The battalion stood in the center of a circle of her friends, a bright smile on her face, feeling surrounded by love and support.

Andy, with a smile on her face, had spoken up, visibly excited.

-"Come on, Maya, let's start with a quick question! What's the biggest secret you've kept from Carina?"

Maya, her cheeks slightly red, had laughed hesitantly. She'd loved these moments of camaraderie, a pleasant contrast to the stress of wedding preparations.

-"Well, I think..." She'd taken a sip of her cocktail, smiling. "...I never told her that I actually hate most romantic movies!"

The friends had burst out laughing, and the light tone had encouraged further revelations.

Victoria, with a mischievous wink, had added:

- "Ah, so we can say you've been fooling Carina with thrillers, then?"

- "As a matter of fact, yes! I prefer suspense to love... Well, for the movies, because obviously I wouldn't trade my future wife and her love for anything in the world!!!" she'd replied, laughing. "But I think she has her own secrets to keep from me!"

The laughter had continued as Maya joked about the movies Carina loved, each comment triggering anecdotes about movie nights spent together. Travis, a former firefighter and friend, had joined the conversation.

Travis, arms crossed with a teasing smile:

- "Seriously, Maya? Even The Notebook?"

Maya, smiling, had shrugged.

- "The Notebook is an exception, but Carina sometimes forces me to watch much worse stuff."

The games had continued, filled with humorous challenges and shared memories. Andy had then proposed a game of «Truth or Consequence», adding a touch of spice to the evening.

- "Maya, truth or consequence!" had said Jack, speaking in a dramatic voice.

- "truth!" Said Maya, with a confident smile.

- "What was your most embarrassing moment with Carina?" Jack asked, his eyes sparkling.

Maya had hesitated for a moment, recalling the many sweet and embarrassing moments they'd shared.

- "I'd say it was the day she caught me singing at the top of my lungs in the shower," she'd finally said, laughing. "She didn't expect me to be so... passionate!" She had said, laughing.

"Oh whatever... If I want to be completely honest I think it was the first time she breastfed in front of me.... I wasn't looking at her in a strange or embarrassing way, but I was completely absorbed by her beauty and this magnificent moment... And I just felt... Completely ashamed, I was this close to telling her I was completely in love with her..."

Her friends laughed, imagining the scene. Amelia, a close friend, added: "Oh, I remember that time you decided to dance like nobody was watching. I had filmed you, and you made me promise never to show it to anyone!"

- "what?! What's all this about?!" Vic asked.

- "I was at the barracks once and Maya was a lieutenant again, and due to an injury she'd been on sick leave, then she'd been put on reception when she got back, so I dropped in unannounced and filmed her through the airlock door... She was dancing like never before... Holding her phone like it was a microphone..."

-"Heyyy! It was a secret!"

- "Is it better here? Or tomorrow at your wedding in front of your wife and all your guests?!"

//

Meanwhile, on the other side of town, in a much calmer atmosphere, Carina, too, was enjoying her hen party. Although the mood was more soothing, the warmth of friendship filled the room, just as it had at Maya's party.

/Carina's Bachelorette Party /

In the tea room and spa where Carina's hen party was taking place, an equally warm atmosphere prevailed. Carina's friends had gathered around a large table, decorated with fresh flowers and scented candles. Tim had taken care to organize everything so that the day would be unforgettable.

-"Well, ladies, this is the first time in my life I've organized a bachelorette party... I hope you'll bear with me!

ready for some pampering?" Asked Tim, with a knowing smile.

The friends cheered, eager to enjoy the spa session.

Gabriella, still full of energy, had thrown in, "I hope there's chocolate somewhere, otherwise I'm not convinced!"

Laughter had erupted as they began to settle in for facials and manicures.

They had all wanted to tell stories they had in common with Carina during their spa session.

- "Once we went on a road trip where we sang at the top of our lungs, and Carina took the steering wheel when she was completely lost. It was so memorable!" Had recounted Tim, who always had a talent for storytelling, and who then began to share an anecdote about a memorable evening they'd spent together with Carina and Danny.

All the girls had started laughing.

"And she was the one who insisted on asking a group of bikers for directions!" he'd added.

Lisa, laughing, had rolled her eyes, "I'd have given anything to see that!"

- "How about a little facial spa before the big day?" The spa employee had interrupted them.

Carina, lying with cucumbers over her eyes, had laughed.

-"If we're going to talk about secrets, I'm ready to spill the beans! The lightness of the atmosphere soothed her mind, allowing her to enjoy these moments before the wedding."

//

While Carina relaxed, Maya reflected on the last years of her life, surrounded by her friends, realizing how much things had changed.

/Maya/

On Maya's side, the mood had intensified. Kate, Maya's sister-in-law at heart, had launched a new activity: a quiz on the bride-to-be. She and Tim hadn't wanted to attend the same bachelorette party.

- "Okay, folks, who really knows Maya?" she challenged. The questions had been funny, ranging from embarrassing anecdotes to the most secret of preferences.

Laughter had erupted as the battalion tried to hide their embarrassment at hearing revelations about her lifestyle habits. From clipboards to protein shakes for breakfast and her former self-care Wednesdays.

The blonde had paused for a moment, observing her friends around her. She smiled sweetly, but her mind wandered to Carina, to their relationship, their future. She wondered how she had come to be here, surrounded by so much love and support.

-"You know," she had said, her voice taking on a more serious note, "I never imagined I'd be here, surrounded by so many people who care about me... It's sometimes crazy to think that a few years ago, I was concentrating solely on my work, that I almost lost most of you... And now I'm getting married."

Her friends had listened attentively, their gazes filled with affection before resuming their various games.

- "Maya, if you had to describe Carina in three words, what would they be?" Jack, a long-time friend, had spoken.

Maya had thought for a moment, her smile widening.

- "Strong, loving and incredibly beautiful. I know it's four words, but «beautiful» isn't strong enough for my fiancée."

The applause had resounded, and a warm feeling enveloped the room. Victoria, seeing her moved, had quickly taken her hand back.

- "Come on, enough emotion, it's time for the next game!" she announced, tossing out charades. Maya's friends launched into hilarious imitations, the blonde clumsily imitating a scene from one of Carina's favorite films, which provoked widespread laughter.

Andy, amused, had declared:

- "If it doesn't work with Carina, I don't know who will!"

//

At the same time, in the soothing calm of the spa, Carina was savoring the sweetness of the moment, surrounded by her friends, ready to raise a glass.

/Carina/

When the spa session was over, Carina stood up to propose a toast.

-"Here's to friendship, love and all the adventures ahead. I'm so grateful to have you in my life."

Glasses had risen, and a sense of joy had filled the room. Friends had shared their favorite memories of Carina, each adding a personal note, strengthening the bond between them.

//

The evening had continued with laughter, games and moments of complicity.

Phyllis, at home with her three grandchildren, listened to their laughter as she prepared a light meal. She knew that these celebrations were essential for Maya and Carina, strengthening the bond between the two families.

Maya and Carina's bachelorette parties created an atmosphere of joyous celebration, full of love and friendship, preparing them both for this new stage in their lives.

As the evening drew to a close in both venues, Maya felt invigorated, surrounded by people who brought her joy and support. For her part, Carina savored every moment spent with her friends, realizing just how precious these moments were.

/Maya/

Maya's evening had unfolded in an electric atmosphere. After a few hours spent in this trendy downtown bar, the group of friends had felt the energy go up another notch. Andy, slightly tipsy but still full of wacky ideas, had raised his glass.

-"Okay, guys, who's up for karaoke? We can't let this evening end so soon!"

Victoria, enthusiastic, had immediately approved:

- "Oh yes, I love that idea! Maya, you're going to show us your hidden singing talents!"

Maya, though a little reluctant to perform, had sensed the general enthusiasm and let herself be carried away by the excitement of the moment.

- "Okay, but I'm warning you, I'm not Céline Dion!"

The group had burst out laughing, each already eager to continue the party elsewhere. They had quickly left the bar, laughing and joking their way through the downtown streets, in search of the famous karaoke recommended by Travis. The night was cool, but the mood was buzzing.

/Carina/

On the other side of town, the atmosphere at Carina's spa/tea was much calmer and more soothing. After the pampering session, the laughter and the softness of the facial masks, Carina's friends began to discuss the next stage of the festivities. Gabriella, full of energy despite the relaxing atmosphere, had launched an idea.

-"You know what, girls? We can't let the evening end here. Why don't we take Carina out for karaoke? That would be the icing on the cake!"

Tim, who had organized the evening, had smiled wryly.

-"I like the idea, Carina's never been the biggest fan of singing in public, but for tonight, I'm sure she'll lend herself to the game."

Carina, her cheeks still pink with care and laughter, had looked up, half-amused, half-worried.

-"Really? Karaoke? You know me, it's not really my thing..."

Lisa, a mischievous smile on her lips, had insisted:

-"That's exactly why it'll be even more fun! Come on, it's your night, let's have some fun!"

Despite Carina's mild protests, the group set off. The city glowed under the night lights, and excitement was slowly building as they approached the venue.

/Karaoke/

The two groups had unknowingly converged on the same karaoke bar. In front of the entrance, Maya and her group had burst out laughing as they discussed the songs they would perform, Maya still unconvinced but ready to play along. As they were about to enter, Victoria had noticed another group coming from across the street.

-"Hey, but wait... isn't that... The future Doctor DeLuca-Bishop?!"

Maya had frozen for a moment, looking surprised, before seeing that yes, indeed, it was her fiancée approaching with her friends, all laughing out loud.

-"No, it can't be..." Maya had smiled, both amused and incredulous.

Andy, with a smile on her face, had raised her arms in victory.

- "Well, I guess we'll be merging our parties!"

Carina's group had noticed them in turn, and the two brides-to-be had exchanged amused glances across the street.

-"You've had the same idea as us, apparently." Said Tim, bursting into laughter, before greeting Maya and her group, his companion included.

Carina, slightly embarrassed, had moved towards Maya, a smile on her lips.

-"I guess we won't have to do two separate karaoke nights..." she said.

- "It's going to be even better," Maya had replied, clearly amused by this happy coincidence.

-"I'm sure I'm going to love watching you sing, sweetheart."

/Band merger /

The evening had taken a whole new turn. The two groups mingled in an explosion of laughter and lively discussion. Maya's friends and Carina's friends had quickly bonded, exchanging anecdotes and jokes as if they'd known each other all their lives.

-"Who'd have thought we'd all end up together tonight? It's a sign, I tell you!" laughed Travis.

-"Or maybe fate wants us all to sing horribly out of tune tonight." Gabriella had replied playfully.

Maya and Carina stood side by side, watching their friends tenderly. They had exchanged a knowing glance, and Maya had then whispered:

-"We couldn't have wished for anything better."

- "Yes," Carina had replied with a smile.

"It's exactly what we needed to round off the day."

The friends had then started choosing songs, the first volunteers had rushed to the stage, while others cheerfully encouraged their performances.

In fact, the girls had all taken to the stage to sing «Man! I Feel Like A Woman!» by Shania Twain.

As for the brides-to-be, they had tried to keep a low profile, but that was without counting on the enthusiasm of their friends.

- "Maya, Carina, it's your turn to sing a duet now!" Andy shouted, always ready to have fun.

- "I don't think that's a good idea..." replied Maya, laughing nervously.

- "Come on, it'll be fun!" insisted Lisa.

Finally, to much encouragement and laughter, Maya and Carina gave in. They got up on stage, slightly nervous but ready to play the game.

After a few seconds' hesitation, Carina had chosen the song «Love The Hell Out Of You» by Lewis Capaldi - a choice that made Maya smile, remembering the birthday party when the brunette had surprised her with this song to dance the two of them in love.

With a smile on her face, the Italian began to sing the first lyrics. But it wasn't long before friendly laughter erupted in the room. Her soft voice was not in the right range, slightly out of tune with the melody.

- "She's a better surgeon than she is a singer, that's for sure!" whispered Gabriella teasingly to Jo.

But Maya, always ready to support her future wife, had waited for the chorus to enter the scene. As soon as she opened her mouth, the room froze. Her voice, clear and powerful, resonated with unexpected accuracy. Laughter fell silent immediately, replaced by raised eyebrows and astonished glances between friends.

- "But... why did she say she made a fool of herself in the shower?" Victoria had whispered, flabbergasted.

- "She sings like a pro..." Tim had nodded, impressed, beside her.

Then a little after the second verse, as the second chorus progressed, Maya had continued to sing with natural ease, her voice now filling the room. And all the while, Carina had just stopped singing, in awe of the beauty of her fiancée's voice. She'd already seen her in all her facets, but this new discovery left her speechless.

Of course, she'd heard her sing before, too few times for her taste, but that was usually in the shower, not on a stage with a microphone. Especially as her companion always deliberately made a fool of herself, feeling uncomfortable in such situations.

Here, tonight, Maya seemed to be made for it, as if, despite her natural gift for being a firefighter, she'd missed her true calling, as if she could have been a singer in another life.

The Italian's eyes filled with wonder, unable to leave her future wife.

The battalion, concentrating on her performance, hadn't immediately realized the effect she was having. Her friends had started to whistle and clap softly to encourage her, but it was Carina's gaze that did most of the talking.

Jo, amazed, had whispered to Gabriella:

- "Looks like she's falling in love with her all over again."

The chorus had become more powerful towards the end, and Carina, moved, had preferred to remain completely silent, leaving Maya to shine alone under the enthusiastic applause and whistles. Not because she didn't want to sing along, but because her fiancée, with that bewitching voice, literally left her speechless.

- "Wow..." Andy had breathed, flabbergasted. "She has the most beautiful voice I've ever heard."

Carina had swallowed, touched by the beauty of the moment. She knew Maya was beautiful, that she was strong and loving, but there, on that stage, singing with such passion and talent, she seemed even more perfect than before. She looked at her as if she were discovering her all over again, even more in love than on the first day.

When the last note rang out, the whole room erupted in cheers and applause. Maya, a little surprised by the reception, had lowered the microphone, slightly embarrassed by so much attention. Her fiancée, on the other hand, had walked slowly towards her, her eyes shining with silent admiration.

-"You're incredible" she whispered, her voice trembling with emotion.

And in the complicity of that moment, the group of friends had erupted in joy, applauding even louder, as if to celebrate not only their love, but this unique connection that had just been exposed through a simple song.

The fusion of the two evenings had finally created an unforgettable memory, blending surprise, love and friendship in a festive atmosphere.

And, as the night wore on, karaoke had turned this happy coincidence into an even more special evening, celebrating not only Maya and Carina's love, but also the strong bonds they shared with their friends.

After spending two hours singing and laughing at the karaoke, Maya and Carina had decided to separate from their friends for the night, aware that they had to get up early the next day for the wedding. In a last burst of friendship, they exchanged promises of happiness and knowing smiles with their friends, before saying good-bye to each other.

The journey back to their home was a quiet one. Tim and Kate, already tired, had taken the lead, while Maya and Carina exchanged gentle glances, looking forward to the events to come. The road, illuminated by streetlamps, had brought a soothing sense of calm.

Once there, they opened the door carefully, so as not to wake the children. Phyllis was asleep in Danielle's room, with little Charlie on a mattress on the floor. Danielle was on the mattress tucked under her brother Andrea's bed, while Andrea slept peacefully in her own bed. The four adults had entered silently, avoiding making any noise, as if they were shadows gliding through the rooms.

Tim and Kate had decided to move into the study on the sofa bed, stretching out comfortably to enjoy a little rest before the big day. Maya and Carina, meanwhile, had headed for their bedroom, where the atmosphere was one of reassuring softness.

Once inside, Maya closed the door behind them, and the outside world seemed to fade away. They had looked into each other's eyes, a glimmer of excitement mixed with apprehension floating in the air.

"We're getting married tomorrow" Carina had whispered, a smile lighting up her face. Her words had floated into space, imbued with the emotion of the moment.

-"Yes", Maya had replied, her voice soft, almost a whisper. She had moved closer to Carina, their hands coming together naturally. "I can't believe it."

They'd sat on the bed, their hands intertwined, their hearts beating in unison. The blonde had then begun to talk about their dreams for the following day, imagining every detail of the ceremony, laughter, and tears of joy. The Italian had listened, absorbing every word with admiration.

The subdued light of the room enveloped the two women in a bubble of warmth and comfort. Maya, her eyes shining, had recalled the moments they had shared, the laughter and challenges that had brought them together.

- "I remember our first meeting, the way you lit up my life without even realizing it while yours was falling apart... you know I walked out of your house, and my first thought was «this is so inappropriate»... I'd just fallen in love for the first time in my life... And with the last person in my life... I can't really say «and for the last time»... Because I fall in love with you every day... "

Carina had smiled, touched by Maya's sincerity. She replied:

-"And I remember every moment that led me to you. I can't imagine my life without you, not now, not ever."

A silence had fallen, comfortable and full of intimacy. The brunette had slipped her head against the blonde's shoulder, breathing in her familiar scent, that of the engraved bottle. Maya had put an arm around Carina's shoulders, holding her close, enjoying the comforting closeness.

"You know," Carina had whispered, "I'm a little nervous."

-"Me too," Maya had confessed, smiling. "But not nervous in a bad way, because I think that as long as we're together, everything will be fine."

Carina had nodded, comforted by her fiancée's presence. They had shared a moment of silence, their hearts beating in unison, full of promises for the following day.

Maya had then risen slightly, leaning in to kiss Carina tenderly. This simple gesture filled the room with a palpable sweetness. In that moment, nothing else mattered. The love they shared seemed to light up the room, chasing away all anxieties.

-"I love you" had whispered the brunette against the blonde's lips, like a silent promise.

-"I love you so much", Maya replied, her heart overflowing with emotion.

They lay together on the bed, entwined, lulled by each other's light breath. Worries had dissipated, replaced by a comforting warmth. Before drifting off to sleep, they shared a few laughs, talking about the day ahead, full of love and complicity.

As they closed their eyes, surrounded by the gentle murmur of the night, they knew that tomorrow would be a memorable day, the start of a new chapter in their lives together.
