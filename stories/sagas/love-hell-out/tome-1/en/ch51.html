It was early November, and Carina and Maya had been engaged for a month. Their happiness was palpable, but life continued to keep them busy with exciting projects. Two weeks earlier, Carina had received an unexpected call from Dr. Laura Dreiser, a researcher she knew well from attending several of her conferences. Laura had been at the forefront of women's health research and often moderated panels on topical issues.

During their conversation, Laura had informed Carina that she had succeeded in booking her a place at an imminent conference in Los Angeles, where she could share the results of her pioneering study into female orgasm. This research had led to the development of a «female Viagra» which would soon be launched on the market, in early 2029. The announcement of this drug had caused a sensation in the medical community, and Laura knew that Carina's voice would be an invaluable asset in raising public awareness of this major breakthrough.

Carina, excited by this opportunity, began preparing her presentation, juggling wedding preparations and her work. She knew that this conference would not only enable her to share her expertise, but also highlight the importance of women's sexual health. Maya, always by her side, encouraged her tenderly, proud of her fiancée's achievements, and it was in fact she who had urged her to go to Los Angeles and told her not to worry, as she would take care of their babies while she was away.

Carina had been invited to speak at the International Women's Health Conference in Los Angeles. She had accepted enthusiastically, proud of the work she had accomplished in recent years, notably with her study of the female orgasm and the development of a «Viagra» for women, a project she had worked on for a long time when Danny was still in this world; and which was finally going to see the light of day. Maya and the children remained in Seattle, Maya taking care of Andrea and Danielle while Carina was away.

A few days before her departure for Los Angeles, Carina had received an unexpected message on Instagram. The name that had appeared on her screen had instantly transported her back to a time she thought was over.

Gabriella Aurora.

Gabriella Aurora: Ciao, Carina,
Spero che tu stia bene. Ho appena visto che sarai alla conferenza di Los Angeles la prossima settimana e sono stata invitata a parlare anch'io! Mi piacerebbe rivederti, magari per una cena dopo la conferenza? Spero che tu sia disponibile!

[Hello Carina,
I hope you're well. I've just seen that you'll be at the LA conference next week, I'm also invited as a speaker! I'd love to see you again, maybe for dinner after the conference? I hope you're available!]

Carina hesitated for a few seconds before answering. It had been years since they'd last spoken. Gabriella had been an important presence in her life when they'd been at university together. They had shared many things, but life had separated them, each going in a different direction. Nevertheless, this proposal of a reunion, after so many years, seemed pleasant, even if they had stopped talking because in a sense Carina had chosen Danny, yet Gabriella seemed to want to wipe the slate clean. Carina had smiled as she typed her reply.

Carina DeLuca: Ciao Gabriella! È da tanto che non ci vediamo! Come te la passi? Sarebbe bello rivederti. Sarò libera dopo l'intervento, quindi possiamo cenare insieme se ti va.

[Hi Gabriella! It's been a while! How have you been? It would be great to see you again. I'll be free after my talk, and we can have dinner together if you like].

The day of the conference had arrived. Carina had taken an early flight to Los Angeles, leaving behind a smiling Maya and the children hugging her tightly before her departure. The journey had given her the opportunity to reflect. She wondered what Gabriella looked like today. Their friendship - or rather their youthful flirtation - seemed distant, almost like another chapter in her life.

Arriving at the hotel, she settled into her room, glancing at her schedule. Her presentation was scheduled for the afternoon, but in the morning she would have time to review her notes. She quickly changed into her professional clothes before heading to the conference center. On the way down to the hall, her phone had vibrated.

Gabriella Aurora : Ci rivediamo stasera dopo la tua presentazione? [We'll meet tonight after your presentation].

Carina answered quickly.

Carina DeLuca: Sì, per me va bene! Finirò verso le 18:00, quindi possiamo cenare subito dopo.

[Yes, that's fine with me! I'll be finished by 6pm, we can have dinner right after.]

When she had entered the large conference room, Carina had felt slightly nervous. She had never loved public speaking, but this project was particularly close to her heart. Her study on female Viagra had been a success, but the obstacles had been many. Now, at last, she was going to speak about it in front of a large audience, among her peers. She already missed Maya, especially knowing that she would have been there to support her if she could.

A few minutes before going on stage, Carina spotted Gabriella at the back of the room. She hadn't changed much, if at all. She still looked as confident as ever, with that charming smile. Gabriella had waved to her, and despite the distance between them, Carina had felt a little twinge of nostalgia and excitement.

-"Ladies and gentlemen, it gives me great pleasure to introduce to you today an exceptional speaker. Dr. Carina DeLuca is an internationally renowned OB-GYN, known for her innovative and passionate approach to the study of women's sexual health. In recent years, she has led a groundbreaking study of female orgasm, aimed at better understanding its effects on the body, particularly in pain management, and exploring solutions for women suffering from sexual dysfunction. This study has also contributed to the development of a new product: a «female Viagra» which will soon be available. It's an honor to have her with us today to share the results of this research. Please give her a warm welcome, Dr. Carina DeLuca!"

- "Thank you, Laura, for that lovely introduction, and thank you all for being here today.

When I started this study a few years ago, my main objective was to better understand the neurological and physiological mechanisms behind female orgasm. But as my research progressed, it became clear that orgasm is much more than a simple biological reaction. It has benefits that are often underestimated, including pain management and the production of oxytocin, a natural hormone that plays a key role not only in intimate relationships, but also during crucial moments such as childbirth.

To carry out this study, I worked with a sample of female volunteers who agreed to masturbate in an MRI machine. This enabled me to observe first-hand the cerebral and physiological reactions during orgasm. I would like to sincerely thank these women for their courage and participation, because without them, this research would not have been possible.

What I discovered is fascinating: orgasm leads to a massive release of oxytocin, which acts as a natural analgesic. This could have important implications for women in labor during childbirth. The idea of promoting oxytocin release in a natural way to reduce pain during childbirth is still being explored, but this study opens the way to new possibilities in pain management, beyond traditional methods.

At the same time, the study also aimed to address an increasingly pressing need among women: finding solutions to sexual dysfunction, particularly hypoactive desire. We know that this problem affects many women, and that there are still too few effective treatments to remedy it. Numerous studies have been carried out on men to develop products to combat sexual dysfunction, including drugs such as Viagra. For a long time, it was thought that such treatments or research could be adapted to women, but this has proved not to be the case. Female and male bodies don't work in the same way, and solutions developed for men don't always meet the specific needs of women. This was one of the central points of my research. Thanks to this research, and with the support of a pharmaceutical company, we developed a product designed to improve sexual desire in women. This «female Viagra», as some like to call it, is based on a better understanding of the hormonal balance and neurological factors that influence desire.

I'm delighted to announce that this product will be available to all women from early 2029. We hope it will help improve the quality of life of those who suffer from this type of sexual dysfunction, and also open the door to future studies on female sexuality, an area that still deserves much more attention.

I would like to thank the pharmaceutical company that has funded this research and supported innovation in this crucial area. Together, we hope to continue advancing women's health worldwide.

Thank you for your attention, and I'm happy to answer any questions you may have."

After finishing her speech, Carina had thanked the audience with a warm smile. She knew that the question part would be just as important, if not more so, for the curious and sometimes critical colleagues. She had taken a deep breath as the first hand was raised in the room.

A blonde-haired woman, elegant in her white blouse, had risen from the ranks.

-"Doctor DeLuca, this is Doctor Lauriane Giachino from Ajaccio in Corsica, France. I'd like to know what prompted you to carry out this study?"

Carina had smiled, taking a moment to recall the start of this long scientific journey.

-"Thank you for the question, Dr. Giachino. What prompted me to carry out this study was a pressing need I had observed in my patients. All too often, I was told of sexual dysfunction or low desire, and I realized that there weren't many solutions, especially for women. The medical industry seemed more interested in male dysfunction, while female sexuality remained a largely untapped field. I wanted to change that. What's more, as an obstetrician, I've long been interested in how hormones and bodily responses can influence key moments like childbirth. The idea of being able to use orgasm to enhance these moments seemed like a promising avenue."

There was a murmur of approval in the room. Another hand had been raised, this time more towards the center.

-"Doctor DeLuca, this is Doctor Marine Kadri from Brussels. I wanted to ask you, why did you choose MRI specifically for this study? Don't you think it might have biased the results with the pressure and discomfort of the participants in such an unnatural setting?"

Carina had expected this question and tilted her head slightly before answering.

-"That's an excellent question, Dr. Kadri. Of course, MRI is not the most comfortable setting, and we were well aware of this from the start of the study. However, it was the only reliable means of observing changes in the participants' brains and bodies during orgasm in real time. We took great care to ensure that the women felt at ease, with clear explanations and a respectful setting. Despite the potential discomfort, the results we obtained were remarkably consistent, showing that the responses observed were genuine. And the feedback from the participants, after having had the opportunity to get used to the machine, was very positive."

Another attentive silence settled in, giving way to the next question.

A woman with black hair pulled up into a bun, seated in the front row, had confidently raised her hand.

-"Doctor DeLuca, Doctor Margot Garcia from Dubai. I wonder if the results you've obtained could also be used in areas other than sexuality or childbirth. Have you considered other potential applications for this natural release of oxytocin?"

Carina had nodded enthusiastically. It was exactly the kind of question she was passionate about.

-"It's an excellent lead, Dr. Garcia. We believe the applications could go far beyond sexuality and childbirth. For example, oxytocin has been studied for its effects on anxiety and depression. We believe that promoting its release in a natural way could have positive repercussions on stress management or even in broader medical contexts, such as chronic pain. We're still in the early stages, but the prospects are vast and promising. It's definitely an avenue we want to explore in future studies."

Carina had glanced at Laura Dreiser, who had given her an encouraging little wave. The exchanges had been fruitful, and she sensed that the audience shared her enthusiasm.

After her successful presentation, Carina joined Gabriella in the lobby of the conference center.

The restaurant Gabriella had chosen was in an upscale part of Los Angeles, far from the hustle and bustle of downtown. It was an intimate place, with a terrace overlooking a garden illuminated by small strings of lights. Carina had been impressed by the choice of venue, elegantly simple but with a warm atmosphere. She followed Gabriella to their table in a quiet corner.

Dinner began slowly, with glasses of white wine and refined amuse-bouche. They had talked about their careers, exchanging anecdotes about their respective experiences. Carina had told some amusing stories from the clinic where she worked, while Gabriella had shared her recent projects as a surgeon specializing in reproductive medicine.

At one point, after a bite of a seafood dish, Gabriella had put down her fork and locked eyes with Carina.

Gabriella had shrugged, a smirk on her face.

Gabriella's tone had made Carina feel slightly uneasy, but she'd quickly shaken it off, not wanting to be distracted by hasty interpretations.

After a moment's silence, Gabriella had asked a more direct question.

-"Tell me, Carina, are you happy in Seattle? With your job, your personal life?"

Carina had taken a sip of wine before answering.

-"Yes, I'm very happy. I'm still as passionate about my work as ever and... my life with Maya and the kids is everything I've ever wanted."

Gabriella had nodded, but the look on her face spoke volumes. She'd leaned forward slightly, her gaze more piercing.

Carina had felt a slight tension rising inside her. Gabriella knew where to press. She herself had often wondered if she was limiting herself to Seattle out of comfort, out of love for Danny, then for Maya and the kids. But was that really all she wanted?

-"I've thought about this a lot, especially over the last few years. I have responsibilities I care about, and right now, I'm exactly where I need to be."

Gabriella had taken a moment to savor one last bite before answering, as if she knew what she was about to say was important.

Gabriella had left a few seconds of silence, scrutinizing Carina's face, hoping to see a spark of buried desire.

"Together, we can do anything. I know you still feel it, that need to create something great. Take back that dream. Make it come alive again. Do it for yourself."

And for the first time since they'd been together, Carina wondered if this ultimate love would be strong enough to survive this temptation.
