She signed autographs and took photos. It seemed so crazy to sign autographs or even take photos with strangers, but here she was, doing it.

 

Without realizing it, she'd spent over three hours just to get out of the airport.
When they'd arrived at her home, she hadn't expected to find her friends and family all there; the welcoming committee had moved her, but she'd started crying for an entirely different reason. The person she wanted to celebrate her victim with wasn't there.

 

Everyone had come to hug her. Grandma Gaga was there, Ruth and Jacob too, Mase, Kathy, Andy and Ryan and all her friends.

 

They'd ordered pizza, nothing like an old ritual. Ollie, Lane and Maya had never once mentioned the Italian girl's name.

 

They'd spent the evening all together, celebrating.

 

When it was time to go to bed, Maya had gone to her brother's room. She didn't feel like sleeping in her studio tonight, and she didn't feel like sleeping in her teenage bedroom either.

 

She had gone to bed in her old double bed, the one that had been Mason's bed since the Italian arrived three years ago, give or take a few days. 

 

She lay facing her brother.

 

-"How was your exhibition?"

 

- "It went really well... I sold one canvas, well three, but the first one was just... The feeling was too strange. I didn't sell for ten thousand dollars, but I sold three hundred dollars each."

 

-"I'm proud of you."

 

-And I'm proud of you. And I know someone else who would have been."

 

Maya had started to cry.

 

-"She was."

 

Mason had raised himself on his elbow.

 

- "Maya?"

 

- "You'll keep it to yourself? No one must know, not even Andy... Carina is... She's studying in London... She came across a poster of me and sent me a message on Messenger... "

 

-"So you've seen Carina?"

 

- "yes... She's got someone."

 

- "oh shit! I'm sorry, I thought that-"

 

- "she... She still loves me, I spent all my free time with her..." She'd blushed.

 

- "spent... spent ?" he asked, raising his eyebrows.

 

- "spent..." She bit her lip despite her tears. "She's even more beautiful than she was two years ago."

 

- "is that possible?"

 

She'd retrieved her phone and gone to a folder with a code. She'd looked for a photo where no part of their bodies was visible and she'd shown it to Mason.

 

- "oh shit, wait, you were naked?"

 

- "look at Carina, not me"

 

- "does that mean yes?"

 

- "Mase yes, I didn't play cards with her"

 

- "no but yes, she's downright sublime" he said, a little too enthusiastic for his sister's taste.

 

-"Calm down, you're only fifteen!"

 

- "soon to be sixteen, and on top of that you were my age when you met her, and you'd already made love with I don't know how many girls!"

 

-"Why did I tell you this?"

 

- "so Carina?"

 

The blonde had a doubt whether to cry or smile, because she was going to be able to talk about herself.

 

- "she sent me a message and I couldn't resist... I invited her to my room that night."

 

- "right on the first night?!"

 

- "no, but it wasn't really our first night together... And I didn't invite her for that purpose... Yes, I knew I was going to want to... But that wasn't why I said yes to her invitation".

 

-"Wait, it wasn't you who invited her?"

 

- "She was the one who asked me to meet her, but I was the one who offered her my hotel..."

 

- "and so?"

 

- "So we talked... You, among other things, and she told me about a guy..."

 

- "a guy?"

 

-"Thomas... And then she wanted to leave... And I walked her to the door... And I asked her if he was her boyfriend... And she said yes... Then she told me he wasn't me and... And I kissed her."

 

- "that's so romantic..."

 

- "maybe, but now... It's over" she had started to cry.

 

- "Maya, you thought it was over and yet here you are and you haven't seen her for two years... It will never be over between you"

 

- "in the meantime, she won't be sleeping in my arms tonight or for the next few nights."

 

- "Maya..."

 

- "excuse me Mase, I'm tired"

 

The blonde had turned to cry and her little brother had come and put his arm around her shoulders and taken her in his arms.

 

She had finally fallen asleep in his arms.

 

The next day, she had a doctor's appointment in Los Angeles. She flew with Ollie and her father. She had indeed sprained her ankle.
There wasn't much she could do. Rest, a bandage and, when she felt better, physiotherapy.

 

They had taken advantage of being in Los Angeles to stay a few nights and she had done several interviews. Newspapers, magazines, TV interviews, lots of interviews that weren't live. Lane was the happiest.

 

He'd taken advantage of the fact that they were there to do some photos for Adidas, as well as for Frosted Mini Wheat.

 

They had returned after almost a week in Los Angeles. The blonde had gone home. She wanted to be able to mourn Carina at last. She'd been holding it all inside for a week.

 

She hadn't left the house for several days. She had cried a lot thinking about the brunette.
She'd been off work as a volunteer firefighter because of her ankle.

 

Almost a week had gone by and Maya had been asked again by her father for interviews. And her birthday was fast approaching. She had gone to Alivia's a few days before her birthday.

 

She had arrived by bus, still unable to drive.

 

-"What's going on?" It was the first thing Grandma Gaga had asked Maya when she saw her.

 

The blonde had burst into tears. They returned to grandma's house.

 

They were sitting at Alivia's.

 

-"And so she said no?"

 

- "She didn't really say no... She... Well, she did ... But not in that way.... Not like that... She just said it's not the right time... She doesn't want to get married to get papers."

 

-"No, then I'm sorry. I love her, but she could have made an effort! She could have come back with you!"

 

- "No, Grandma, I know she loves me and I know why she couldn't 'come' with me... I just... It doesn't work that way. "

 

- "but if she loves you-"

 

- "yes, but if we go that way... I could have stayed with her in London 'if I loved her'."

 

- "but you love her"

 

-"more than anything else in the world. I'd swap my Olympic medal for her in a heartbeat".

 

-"yes, but you wouldn't go and live in London, you don't know anyone there, your whole family's here".

 

-"and all her family is in Italy".

 

-"Yes, but we're all here. She's got lots of friends here".

 

- "that's not how it works... Do you... Do you want to see a picture of her? I mean... A recent photo?"

 

- "with pleasure"

 

Maya had taken her phone from her pocket and shown Alivia a photo where they were both dressed, compared to the photo she'd shown her brother.

 

-"Oh my God! But she's even more beautiful than she was two years ago."

 

-"you have no idea... She's gorgeous, I think the more time goes by, the more beautiful she gets".

 

While she was at her grandmother's, the day after her birthday, her phone rang.

 

-"Hello?"

 

- "Maya Bishop?"

 

- "yes..."

 

-"Hello, this is Captain Robert Sullivan. I'm in charge of the Seattle Fire Academy, and I've received your application. "

 

- "my application?"

 

-"Yes, it was a bit late, but I couldn't possibly turn down an Olympic champion. And I know about your sprain, we'll be lenient with you for the first few weeks".

 

- "my application?"

 

- "you didn't apply?"

 

- "I, I had to ... But I didn't take the time".

 

- "you must have a guardian angel then"

 

- "Ollie..."

 

- "Ollie?"

 

- "can you do me a favor?"

 

- "what do you mean?"

 

- "my best friend would like to become a firefighter too, we're volunteer firefighters together-"

 

- "who's your friend?"

 

- "Andrea Herrera"

 

- "Pruitt Herrera's daughter?"

 

- "yes"

 

- "tell her to come and see me tomorrow at nine o'clock, I'll interview her"

 

- "and me?"

 

- "you're pretty well known and I've asked for your service record at Station 19 you've got good results. You'll prove yourself straight away when you join the academy".

 

- "When's the new school year?"

 

-"September third."

 

- "there's a possibility that I'll have to take some time off for interviews and thing like that."

 

-"No worries. The SFD brass were impressed that the reigning Olympic champion wanted to become a firefighter in our city. They could give you anything."

 

- "Thank you, Captain"

 

-"and tell your friend that I'll expect her tomorrow at nine o'clock".

 

- "without fail"

 

Maya had immediately called her best friend.

 

- "tell me you're home!" These were Andy's first words

 

- "better than this?"

 

- "are we moving in together?"

 

- "surprisingly, that might be the case"

 

- "meaning?"

 

- "tomorrow at nine o'clock you have an appointment with Captain Sullivan at the Seattle Fire Academy"

 

- "huh?!"

 

- "he just called me, and it seems my application was accepted... I'm starting on September the third... And you have an interview tomorrow"

 

- "remember I'm going to Seattle University?"

 

- "and remember you don't like it and want to be a firefighter ?"

 

- "wait... Really? I actually got an interview after I applied?"

 

- "yes"

 

- "I love you!"

 

- "I love you too"

 

- "you didn't even tell me you'd applied!" Andy added accusingly

 

- "that's probably Ollie, I didn't take the time to do it"

 

- "oh okay! And when are you coming home? Because it's been two years since you'd rather have your birthday with Alivia than me!"

 

-"I'll be back tomorrow. Besides, I'm starting physio".

 

-"I miss you".

 

- "miss you too"

 

- "see you tomorrow?"

 

-"of course, you have to tell me about your interview".

 

- "and you London. I haven't been able to have you all to myself, since you came home!!!"

 

Maybe Granny Gaga was right. She was Maya's best friend. She'd been spending her birthday with her for two years. And the blonde had been able to tell her about Carina, but she couldn't talk about it with Andy already two people knew.

 

It was time for her to go back to Seattle. She'd spent her week talking to Alivia about Carina, so she'd spent her week crying. At least being with people who didn't know she'd seen Carina meant she wouldn't have to talk about it, which meant she wouldn't have to think about her too much, which meant she wouldn't have to cry too much.

 

She could see herself two years back, only this time she had to hide everything. No one would understand her relapsing into depression when she'd learned to live with the brunette's absence.
She had learned to love the silence of the night because it allowed her to think of Carina, to mourn her and to remember that she had been in her arms again. The emotions in her body were more than contradictory; she didn't know what to feel.

 

If she'd managed to see Carina again after two years, then maybe hope wasn't lost.
She lay down one last time, for this week, in the bed she had once shared with the brunette at her grandmother's house. They had enjoyed their last evening together.

 

She took the embroidered sweater she'd picked up in her studio after LA and rolled it into a ball under her nose.

 

-"Good day my love" she said as she went to bed at midnight, knowing that it was eight o'clock in the morning in London. She had closed her eyes before adding: "Ti amo per sempre".
