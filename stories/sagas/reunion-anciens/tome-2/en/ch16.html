I can't repeat it enough, but everything is perfect... just like you..."

The blonde had slipped one of her hands up Carina's lower back to hold her against her, and her other arm had come up against her spine and she'd come to hold the Italian's neck to kiss her.

A few more dances had passed, with Maya and Carina still on the dance floor in love with their guests. Then the host invited the blonde to announce the next and final entertainment of the evening.

She took the brunette by the hand and they climbed to the roof, followed by their guests.

-"Maya?!" Carina asked, as if to indicate that she thought she'd understood and that the brunette was completely mad.

They had climbed to the roof of the building, followed by their guests, and from where they stood they had a view of the port. It wasn't long before the first fireworks went off, while Carina was in her wife's arms, just like the other way round earlier in the evening, when they'd both slipped away.

That had been the highlight of the evening. From now on, the evening would be nothing but dancing and drinks to last the rest of the night.

After a magnificent fireworks display, everyone had gone back downstairs.

-"You know, Maya, you paid for all of Seattle to have fireworks!" Dean laughed.

-"That's okay, it was worth every dollar I spent to see the wonder on my wife's face."

Maya's firefighters had during both ceremonies worn their A-Classes, but for the evening they were all in civvies.

When they had all reached the first floor again, hot drinks and soft drinks and spirits were on the table at the end of the dance floor. Beers, digestifs, Coca-Cola, coffee, tea - there was something for everyone. To accompany the drinks bar, a candy bar had also been set up on the same table. The photographer took a few photos of the candy bar before leaving the party.

Lucia had taken advantage of the rush to get sweets and drinks to come and talk to the newlyweds.

-"Maya, my dearest wish has always been to see my children happy, in love and loved unconditionally. I can't believe everything I've seen today and tonight. The love you have for my daughter goes beyond anything we can imagine. I could never have dreamed of a better daughter-in-law, a better daughter and a better wife for my baby.

I know that with you she will be happy and I can't wait for you to expand this family."

-"Thank you Lucia. Everyone congratulates me, but what no one seems to realize is that Carina is my muse, without her I wouldn't have been able to do all this, all this organization, all this preparation, to have all these ideas and put everything into making them come true. A few hours after asking me to marry her, she told me she couldn't wait any longer, that when we got back from Sicily she wanted us to get married... love gives you wings, and without her love, without the love I have for her, I'd never have been able to do all this in the time I had.

Just like the fact that I know that without her I wouldn't have won this medal. It's Carina who pushes me to be the best version of myself, because I want her to be proud of me, I want to impress her every day of our lives".

-"I am, Maya. I've always been impressed by you and you continue to do so every day. You never cease to amaze me."

- "Keep loving each other this way. Keep shining for each other. Show each other your love day after day. For I have never seen a purer, more sincere and more beautiful love than the one you have for each other. I love you my children.

- "ti amo mamma"

- "ti amo anch'io Lucia"

For once, they were back to hugging.

Carina had then dragged Maya towards the candy bar before heading out onto the dance floor to join their friends and family.

At nearly three o'clock in the morning, the blonde came to speak in her wife's ear.

- "my love... don't you think it's time... I mean... for you and me to go and celebrate our union... alone..."

- "if I think it's time for my wife and I to slip away, because I've wanted since I was seventeen to make love to my wife... with the honor of calling her that..."

They had stopped by to embrace all their guests, thanking them for coming and inviting them to stay until whatever time he wanted. Brunch would be served at one o'clock and they would return at that time. In the meantime, everyone could stay and enjoy the party.

They had wished good luck to the Maya firefighters who were working in a few hours.

They were out the door and the limousine was waiting for them again, as Maya had contacted the driver after suggesting that her wife slip away and before saying good-bye to everyone.

The blonde had hurried to open the door and waved the driver off.

She had held out her hand for Carina to take:

- "Mrs. DeLuca-Bishop, if you'd be so kind as to get into your carriage..."
