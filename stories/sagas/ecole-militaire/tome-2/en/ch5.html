But in the meantime, they'd been making love for hours. The Italian had bitten her lip, blushing, this night was truly perfect, epic, and they'd even cried while making love. She'd run her fingers over her lips, still feeling the sensation of Maya's lips, tongue and teeth against them. A huge smile had formed on her mouth: she and Maya, her Maya, had made love after one thousand five hundred and forty-eight days, thirty-seven thousand one hundred and fifty-two hours.

She wanted to see her, she heard noises in the kitchen. She had looked at the clothes at the end of the bed and leaned forward to pick them up. She had hesitated to join her naked. She brought the clothes to her nose, they smelled of Maya. She'd closed her eyes and hesitated, no, it was the blonde's turn to make the next move, and besides, these clothes smelled too much like the firefighter for her not to put them on. She had smiled and closed her eyes one last time to smell the blonde's clothes. Maya wanted Carina to wear her clothes after they'd made love all night, and if she hadn't been afraid of being overheard by the lieutenant, she'd have sunk back into bed to slap her legs and hands on the mattress and howl with joy.

She finally got up and got dressed before going into the kitchen to join the blonde who was making pancakes. One of her hands rested on her belly while the other came up against her sternum, she felt the necklace and ring under her T-shirt and Maya's heart went into extrasystole.

-"buongiorno amore della mia vita".

She had kissed the back of her neck before caressing her nose from the nape of her neck to the middle of the back of her head to breathe in her scent. She already had to restrain her hands from caressing the blonde, from slipping under her clothes to touch her skin.

Then she'd felt the blonde stiffen, she hadn't really understood her body's reaction, why did Maya seem to be back in «before last night» mode?

-''h-h-hello''

The wedding ring was still in place, not where she would have preferred to see it, but it was back around the lieutenant's neck, so that meant she still loved her, but why stiffen, why not return her words of love, her nickname or even her embrace?

Carina was thinking that the proposal she'd hoped for when she woke up wasn't likely to come any time soon. Maybe tonight was too soon for Maya after all, maybe they should have waited, again.

She released her embrace, retrieved her phone from her purse in the hallway and went to sit on a stool on the island while the blonde finished preparing breakfast for them. She had been looking at the photos that had been posted on Instagram stories last night by their friends and the photos she herself had taken with her phone. She'd lingered over a photo her twin had sent her where she and Maya were talking very closely, she had her finger threaded through the opening of her shirt between two buttons and Maya was looking at her lips as they spoke, even in profile and through a photo, Carina could feel the blonde's desire for her. She had raised her eyes to look at the Maya currently in front of her, from behind, before lowering her eyes back down to this photo. She couldn't help but wonder «where did last night's Maya go, the one from last night?»
