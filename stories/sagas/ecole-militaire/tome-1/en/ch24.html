Carina was in fetal position on the tarmac of the base. Andrea who had foreseen the blow was standing not far from there. Not that he had wanted to spy on his sister but he knew her. He knew that she would not go well.

He approached and gently placed his hand on the back of his sister: "Carina, Carina come you, you will catch cold".

- "she, she is gone" she said with a sob

- "I know... Come on."

All of Carina's energy had evaporated. Just as Maya had predicted, only a fleshy shell remained. Carina was no longer there. At least not the smiling, charming, playful Carina they all loved. Only the sad and depressed Carina remained, a lifeless Carina.

She didn't even know how her legs had managed to get to her house, but now she was in bed, the pillow Maya had slept on in her arms, pressed against her heart. Flooded with tears.

Andrea knocked on the door and entered.
The last person to do this was Maya, when she had arrived three days earlier with the worst news. Andrea was holding a package in his hands.

-"it's... it's... from Maya" He spoke in a quiet, unsure voice, not knowing what his sister's reaction would be.

She got up at three quarters. Wiped with a backhand a tear that threatened to join the others on the pillow. She sniffed: "what is it?"

-"I, I don't know Maya gave me this for you, to give to you after she left."

- "but when?"

-"Friday night"

She opened the package. Some things had been put there months before. Maya knew that there was a chance that they wouldn't stay together on the same base forever, so she had been putting in memories for the day when one of them would leave.

There were movie tickets, one for each, Maya had to keep the second one for herself. A package of marshmallows. A t-shirt with her scent on it, this one was recent, a bottle of her perfume, a teddy bear, a USB key and photos, photos of them, photos of Carina she didn't know existed and a love letter.

Carina plugged the USB key into her computer which was next to her.

There were several things. Videos, photos, songs, and a "to my love" album. Carina decided to start with that one. It was full of videos. She opened the first one titled "Day 1". Maya was on the screen talking to the camera.

- "Hi, I don't know if this is going to lead to anything but last night Carina and I made out. It was for a game, but I got chills, I've never felt like this before. I think that's a good thing, isn't it? If it wasn't for Jack... I could have kept kissing her. She's so beautiful. I can't stop thinking about her and that kiss."

She decided to open another video, not necessarily tracking the days. "Day 7."

- "this girl drives me crazy, but why hasn't she kissed me? Maybe she just wants to see if I like her. Maybe she's waiting for me to kiss her. And her smeeeeell" she said as she threw herself back on her bed before sitting back down. "She's driving me crazy, I need to know if she likes me. I could have sworn she was going to kiss me goodnight"

It was, like a diary, a diary of Maya's perspective on their story.
She opened another video.

- "She kissed me," she said ecstatically. "We're together. She liked me, she's so perfect."

Then another video.

- "I'm not going to last long, I want her so much. I've never felt like this before. I... I think I love her. That must be it, she haunts my thoughts, she's all I think about day and night, she's all I talk about. Have I talked to you about anything else since school started? I love her" she had just realized the video was still running with a frozen Maya.

She opened another video.

-"diary, this third date was great, Etna, the house and ... The sex! It was, wow epic, she, she really, really is...." she says as she puts her face to the camera "very good. It was the best sex of my life. Oh and uh... I might have told her I loved her, bye!"

There were over 300 videos like this. Maya had talked to her camera almost every day. And she shared them with Carina, so she could see how much she loved her.

At the end of the file there was another "before you" file. It was again videos. With dates this time.

September 5th :
-"hello, tomorrow is the first day of school, I'm in no hurry. I haven't made any friends and anyway I have Mason. I miss France, I miss the United States. They say that Italy is the country of beautiful girls, one thing is sure, they don't hide here".

September 6:
- "Forget everything I said yesterday. My office mate, she's Italian and my gosh, I think she's a goddess. She is beautiful, long curly brown hair, a look, omg her eyes! ... the most radiant smile I have ever seen and a tanned skin. Sublime, there is no other word, sublime. The only thing is to know if she likes girls, well I don't care if she likes girls, I want her to like me. All the guys hit on her. I don't like it. But I understand them. Gentlemen, you have competition in town" she says to the camera in her most serious tone.

Most of the videos brought laughter to Carina's body or at least smiles. Tears too.

She picked up the teddy bear that smelled of Maya's perfume and held it close to her heart as she watched the rest of the videos in chronological order. She still didn't know how well she would know these videos by heart even years later.

When she clutched the teddy bear Maya's voice came out: "I love you amore Mio, per sempre. I miss you so much".

She pressed the teddy bear several times and then resumed playing the videos.

She sent several messages to Maya. Her reactions to the videos. But also thoughts. Words of love. Maya would surely be happy to read them when she landed on her continent.

At 10pm Carina received several messages from Maya. She had just landed after a 15 hour flight. In Arizona the day was still young, it was only 1pm.

Unfortunately Carina had finally fallen asleep, washed away by all her tears.

At 1am she woke up and saw messages from her girlfriend. She decided to call her on video.

They exchanged a few words, about the flight, the gifts... But mainly they looked at each other. One in the night and the other in the day. 1am at one place and 4pm at the other.

Carina eventually fell back asleep looking at Maya on her phone.

The days went by and were similar. They called each other several times a day, sometimes during the lunch break if their schedules matched. Or at times when one of them couldn't. Carina had become rather lonely. Sometimes mixing with others but often alone anyway.

Carina listened to very little music but one was playing on a loop several times a day on the playlist she had made: "la solitudine" by Laura Pausini.

It had been three weeks since Maya had left. Three more weeks and Carina could join her in Arizona. She would leave the day after Christmas and return the Sunday before school started.
Vincenzo saw his daughter's pain and agreed that she would not go to school the day after winter break ended.

The two girls had planned everything. And Maya was ready to receive her beauty.
