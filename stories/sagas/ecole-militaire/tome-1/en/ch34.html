One would have thought that after more than a year they would have gotten used to living away from each other but the reality was quite different.

Carina often woke up in the middle of the night sweating, having had a nightmare that Maya had been killed during a mission and that she would never see her again. 
She never told anyone about these dreams.

She knew Maya wasn't gone yet, but ever since the day Maya left a year ago and the announcement that she was going to military school and then on a mission, the closer the deadline came, the more she felt it in her gut. There was nothing she could do about it. So she wrote.

She wrote about all the feelings of pain that went through her body. She wrote about her nightmares, about her fears, about the pain she felt about having her love away from her.

Of course she would cry on the phone after a hard day, but she didn't want to cry on the phone, she didn't want Maya to blame herself, because it wasn't her fault, she didn't make the decision to leave. No, she wanted to hold Maya but that was impossible.

Andrea saw it well, his sister had changed, she had become sad, in a general way, when Maya was not around, the light of the beautiful Italian evaporated. He did not like to see it thus, nobody liked to see it thus, her, which certainly before Maya was reserved, was now reserved and sad.

The first few nights when one of them had to go home were the most difficult. After spending 4 days or a month sleeping together the pain was just as intense. They could convince themselves for days that the smell of the other persisted on a shirt or a pillow, until they resigned themselves and washed them. This was surely the most painful feeling and moment, the emptiness afterwards. The lack, the loneliness after one of them is gone. It was in these moments that they stayed the most time cloistered in their respective rooms, going out only to go to class. Or to eat a little. Everything seemed bland to them, even the blue of the sky or the warm wind that caressed their faces. This feeling would fade after two or three weeks. But deep down it was still there. It was just less noticeable or a feeling of habit had taken over.

The Italian was doing her best to hide it to Maya because she had made a promise to her but this last one was not fooled and then she could not blame Carina. She also woke up at night, she was having anxiety attacks, about Mason, about her Italian, about a lot of things and only the latter knew how to calm her anxiety attacks, but she was far away and powerless. She could not thus force herself to reveal it to her.

The first separation had been the most painful but also the least painful.

It was the most painful because it was sudden, everything had happened in three days. After finding Carina on the tarmac, Andrea had been there for her. And the videos of Maya that she was still watching months and months, even years, later had been a big part of it.

But at one point it wasn't that he had stopped being there, but Carina was putting on a good face, among other things for her, and she knew she would see her again. She didn't want to bother others with her 'problems'. And her brother was happy with Sam. She didn't want to spoil that for him. 
That didn't stop her from sometimes going to her brother's bed at night, snuggling up to him, crying and falling asleep. It was one of her two favorite pairs of arms in this world.

For Maya it had been the same. Arriving during the year in a country where she had not been for 5 years. In a state she didn't know. With people she didn't know, without Mason. Alone, alone in the world. And yet what tore at her heartstrings the most was having to leave Carina. She could have lived decently in a new place alone, as long as Carina was there everything would have been fine.

They had spent 14 months together and now nothing. Sometimes she imagined that Carina would walk through the door and make her own little face when she said or did something wrong. 
She loved it when she did that. It was one of the reasons she loved her.

She had no video of Carina talking to the camera about how she felt about her. It had all happened so fast. But she did have their pictures plastered above her bed, around her mirror and here and there. And videos they had made together during their excursions or parties. 
She had even received videos over time from their mutual friends who had filmed them when they weren't looking, dancing together, kissing or just looking at each other and chatting.

She was withdrawn. When she wasn't with Vic or on the phone with her girlfriend. She was in her gym venting all her frustration. She had told Carina that her muscles had grown because they had so much training in her new class, but in reality she could spend hours running on a treadmill or on the base athletic field. 
She figured that if she ran fast, faster and faster, she could create a time-space rift that would lead her back to Carina. Or maybe it was her passion for DC comics that put these ideas in her head.

With each separation, the closer the days of meeting again came, the more they felt alive again. They were talking about nothing else, they were eating properly again. Their loved ones could see their behavior change and improve day by day. It was an endless loop, the departure, the habit and the excitement.

The second separation was for Carina one of the worst, she had left Arizona at the beginning of January and was not supposed to see Maya again until June. This was without taking into account the surprise that her blonde had made to her to come to spring break.

The timing of this visit was perfect, as much as when they were physically together the headaches were trivial or insignificant, but when they were separated, the tension rose.

Carina couldn't stand that Maya was in a military school that was sucking up all her free time and energy. They sometimes had trouble communicating, but they didn't do it on purpose. These tensions between them were nothing more than an expression of their common frustration over their forced separation.

Maya was the most sanguine, she was easily irritated for not much and especially in one go. Carina had more of a fiery temperament. Her Italian side came out in those moments.

Sometimes they would yell at each other on the phone, Carina mostly in Italian. They would hang up and call back within 30 seconds to apologize and say they loved each other 'per sempre'.

Their arguments could range from a T-shirt that was too tight to someone who spoke to them, to a whole range of other reasons.

Since she was no longer around, Carina was being hit on again, mainly by those who didn't know Maya. She didn't understand that despite the fact that she looked like she was on the verge of collapse, boys still found her so attractive to come and accost her. Besides, it wasn't always her who told Maya. But sometimes Travis or one of the other guys would slip up when they talked to Maya about someone who was insistent. The goal was not to antagonize the Italian, but Maya's anger won out. So yes, when Amelia had gotten closer to Carina, the blonde had not liked it. She had been suspicious and had been right.

But the words went beyond the thought. Sometimes they said horrible things just to hurt each other. Maya especially had this flaw. To say that maybe she had encouraged them to come to her. Maybe she didn't miss Maya that much.

In reality she knew that what she often reproached the brunette for was stupid and unfounded. But, and this went both ways, arguing made their relationship and their pain more real. They needed that sometimes to feel alive.

Obviously it was not every day, only when the pain became unbearable.

The third separation was not planned as it should have been the second. But strangely enough it was probably the least painful. Carina was ecstatic to have seen Maya. She couldn't believe how romantic her girlfriend was. Of course she was sad that she had to leave, but she was still on cloud nine. This visit was unexpected, but it had come to break up a five and a half month separation.

They couldn't stop thinking about each other 24 hours a day. Any excuse was good to send messages, a funny cloud, a hard exam, a song on the radio, anything. Often it was just to say I love you and how much they missed each other.

They had to make the contest of the most depressing playlist because listening to Celine Dion, Mariah Carey, Creep by Radiohead, breath me by Sia, among others had become their top spofity to both.

Their respective friends and relatives could see this suffering on a daily basis, but they were all powerless in the face of their pain. Nothing they could say or do would ease the fire that consumed them from within. 
So they all waited patiently for time to pass and for them to see each other again. 
This was also one of the reasons why their parents were willing to give them anything. Anything that could make their daughters smile again was good for them.

An expensive plane ticket? A trip to the other side of the world? 3 weeks away from them while they were minors, everything as long as the spark of their heart was alive again.

The four months that preceded Maya's impromptu arrival had probably been the worst so far. Four months that should have been five.

After the second month and a half they became irritable. Not all the time but at times. Irritable with others, their parents, their teachers, other kids. The slightest event could send them into a tailspin.

Maya had taken up boxing as a way of venting and Carina had immersed herself in her studies and work. They were doing their best not to explode at the slightest opportunity. But seeing Vic happy, their parents happy, Andrea happy, Arizona and Callie planning their wedding... Only made their pain more intense. Why them, why, what had they done to deserve this. Was it life that was testing them. Maya had never regretted so much having to do her 6 months of internship. Six months of immersion, without permission. Without seeing her beautiful. These 6 months were going to be the worst of her life, she knew it and she wasn't there yet. And those 6 months were fast approaching. It was the beginning of December and she had to leave in August for her mission.

Between Thanksgiving and Maya's departure on her mission they would see each other three times. At Christmas, in the spring and the next summer. 
And then they wouldn't see each other again until February of the following year. This separation would undoubtedly be the most painful and they didn't know how much yet.

They had gotten into the habit of writing letters to each other. They got into the habit because when Maya was away on a mission, this would be their only means of communication. So once every one to two weeks they would send each other letters. Sometimes with gifts.

Now they would see each other again very soon. But that did not make the distance any less difficult. It was so easy to get used to each other's presence. 
This time, hopefully, they would see each other again in two weeks.
