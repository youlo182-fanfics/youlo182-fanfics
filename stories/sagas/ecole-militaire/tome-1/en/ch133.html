132 chapters, a story of magnificent love, fusional, passionate, timeless and solid in the face of life's trials. All these trials that make us who we are, that justify our choices and that the other accepts unconditionally and that help us to get through them and to get up again.
The story of two people who are perfect for each other, complementary, that nothing and no one could have prevented from loving each other, of an unconditional love, soul mates.
A story full of lessons, whether it is trust, love of oneself and of others, to abandon oneself to each other without conditions.

It made us feel so many emotions, I have never laughed so much, cried so much or been so touched by a story. The wedding was so beautiful that many of us lost the words.

This story brought us closer to each other and created friendships across France, of all ages, without which this would not have been possible. Thanks to this story I got to know the Spice Girls. I can also say that I had a real love at first sight, a sincere friendship was born and I found my 'binôme' so just for that... THANK YOU.

We were there to read a story about Carina and Maya and we found so much more so thank you @youlo182 for your remarkable work, you have gold at your fingertips, this way of writing, of transcribing emotions and your very "graphic" writing ... You always added your little touch like the pictures, where you always made the effort to look for them in relation to the chapter. You always paid attention to the smallest detail and nothing was left to chance, even the songs, always chosen with care and taking the time to translate them. All these letters, these declarations, each one more beautiful and touching than the other... heartbreaking letters for the most part but so beautiful that I enjoyed reading them from the first to the last. They gave off so much pain but also and especially so much love. You always stayed true to the series and the original characters and that's what I liked about it, despite the different starting point, we were able to find them. You captured them so well that you even anticipated their story in Station 19.

It was a real pleasure to play the assistant for this story that brought me so much, that I will miss enormously and also to have had the honor and the privilege to write this afterword. But my greatest pleasure will have been to read it, some chapters will have been very moving, some epic but all will have been exceptional and magnificent.
A story that I could read again and again.

Banane3009
