The vacations had been rather quiet for Carina. She had been working, sending letters to Maya every few days depending on what she had to tell her, and sometimes even sending pictures. They had quickly decided to number the envelopes, they might receive several at the same time.

She still didn't know what part of the world Maya was in. According to the letters she received Maya had gone to a really hostile country but she didn't know how hostile.

For Maya it was complicated, she was a woman, in a country that was hostile to women, to Americans, and to many people in general.

The letters she received from Carina were her lifeline, her escape. And even then, she stayed mainly at the base or only went on reconnaissance missions. But these missions were sometimes attacked.

She couldn't tell Carina where she was, but she could write her. Write her how she felt, tell her that she missed her. She had to restrain herself from reading all the letters at once. Mail delivery was not a daily occurrence. Sometimes the urge was stronger than she was and she would just read the letters over and over, until she knew them by heart. Until she imagined Carina writing them. Or just imagining Carina living the moments described.

the Italian woman had sent letters to Maya every day. Every day she begged her to answer. The last fifteen days she had written home directly, knowing that the army system would take a fortnight to deliver them.

Maya received Carina's letters well. Sometimes she read them, sometimes she didn't. It was too painful. Her brother was everything to her. She had made up her mind, she would not join the army, she could not risk her parents losing another child to the war.

She loved Carina but she couldn't go back to her. Not after all this not after losing her brother. Not after all her trials. Those trials that had changed her. Not that she didn't still love Carina because she loved her, more than anything, more than her own life. But she refused to come back to Carina and be the destroyed girl she was today. This destruction she was partly responsible for. She who had this dream of becoming a soldier. The death of her brother was not her fault, but wanting to follow in the footsteps of her brother and father was. And she was angry with herself.

She couldn't go back to Carina, not because she didn't love her anymore, or less, simply because she couldn't keep the promise she had made to her. She was supposed to be back in Arizona in a fortnight. It had been two and a half months since she had sent a letter to Carina. Not that she hadn't written one. But that she hadn't sent one.

That phrase from Carina kept echoing in her head 'come back to me, come back to me as you are here'. But that was it, that Maya "there" was no longer there. Her brother's death and this experience in a war-torn country had broken her. She could never be the Maya that Carina had loved. She could never be that carefree, happy girl again.

Carina belonged to her former life. Letter 47 was the last letter Carina had received. A last letter. One last time.
