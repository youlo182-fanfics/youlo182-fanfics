Several days had passed and Maya's condition had not improved.

Every day she underwent clinical examinations. Neurological examinations several times a day with Glasgow scores.

His vital signs, pulse, blood pressure, respiratory rate, temperature, were taken every two hours. As well as biological check-ups, blood ionograms, renal function haemograms, among others. And daily radiology of the thorax.

But also regular research of complications, skin examination, eye and mouth examination, perfusion sites, venous condition. And a monitoring of the inputs, liquid and caloric intakes, as well as the outputs, urine bag, and finally its nutritional status.

She was also on a strict regimen. Water and ion intake, according to blood and urine ionograms, and input and output balance. Caloric intake by gastric tube or parenteral, intravenous, of about 2000 calories per day, to be adapted to her nutritional status. But also vitamin B1, B6, PP, and phosphorus.

She had been placed under mechanical ventilation, in case of respiratory failure, with frequent aspiration of bronchial secretions. In the room where she had been placed, everything had been arranged, anti-bedsore mattress for example. She received skin care and monitoring of the pressure points through regular massages. But also eye care and mouth care.

Finally, she had the right to passive physical therapy, in bed, to prevent tendon retractions. And finally, prevention of venous thrombosis by heparin therapy.

Carina came every day, before, after work and on her break. Sometimes she even came to see her fiancée during her work hours. Maya was rarely alone. If she wasn't with the brunette, Vic, Andy or another firefighter would come to keep her company.

Many of the Italian's things were now in her office or in her locker. Lieutenant Herrera would come and bring her clothes and bring back the dirty ones to clean them.

Carina rarely left the hospital. She showered in the hospital, slept in Maya's room on a couch almost every night, or slept in one of the rest rooms, dedicated to doctors, in case the blonde woke up.

They had been engaged for two months now. The wedding should take place in five months, if Maya woke up in time. She talked to her hoping to get a reaction from the love of her life.

Tonight she had joined Maya in her room once again. Vic had come by to bring her food. They all knew that Carina rarely left the hospital and the food was not the best in the world.

Once Hughes left, the Italian settled down beside her fiancée. She took to her the hand which did not have the broken wrist.
