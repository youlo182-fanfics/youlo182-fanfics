Once again, I have the honor of doing the afterword for this second story which is as beautiful and full of feeling as the military school but also more tortured and so hard to read at times.

And that's what they both chose and it was the best choice of their lives. Thanks to their love, they were able to overcome all the trials that life has inflicted on them, against all odds...

Thanks again to @youlo182 for giving us this incredible story. To have made us laugh, cry, be afraid and to transmit so much emotion is not an easy thing to do but she succeeded wonderfully. And I'm a witness to say that she cares about it in every chapter and just for that thank you.
