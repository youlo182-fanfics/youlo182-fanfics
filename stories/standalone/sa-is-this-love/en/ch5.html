"-And to Belpasso, who gave birth to the most beautiful woman I'll ever know."

Carina had shaken her head, feigning exasperation, but the light red that had tinged her cheeks betrayed just how much Maya knew how to touch her.

After dinner, they had ventured into the lively alleyways of Chiaia, where the nocturnal energy of Naples seemed to vibrate in the air. Live music, lively conversation and laughter were all around them as they strolled hand in hand.

The day ended in a burst of light and tenderness, leaving behind an unspoken promise to continue exploring the world and their hearts together.

Carina and Maya had woken up late that morning, savoring their last day in Naples with a sense of quietude. After the hustle and bustle of the first two days, they had decided to start the morning quietly, enjoying the gentle sea air.

Carina, as a passionate guide, had taken a moment to explain to Maya the history of the castle.

"You know, sweetheart," the Italian had begun, biting into a fried pizza: "Neapolitans take their fritto very seriously. Every little detail, every ingredient is important."

After this immersion in the life of Naples, they had decided to enjoy a little peace and quiet. They headed for the Parco Virgiliano, a park overlooking the Bay of Naples. The almost secret location was ideal for relaxing before leaving the city. As they strolled along the pine- and bougainvillea-lined paths, they had come face to face with a breathtaking view of the sea, the islands of Capri, Ischia and Procida glittering in the distance.

"Look..." had said Carina, pointing to the distant islands. "This park was created to offer the best view of the whole bay. This is where Virgil, the Roman poet, used to come to recharge his batteries. You can imagine, in those days, the peace and quiet he must have felt."

Maya, who couldn't resist taking out her phone to snap a few photos, had captured this moment when the sea mingled with the deep blue sky, creating a peaceful canvas of light and nature. -"I've never seen a place like this..." said the blonde, her eyes shining. "It's perfect, it's... how shall I put it... a fitting end to this Neapolitan adventure."

"remember this is the wine I told you about when we went to the volcano..."

- "yes, Lacryma Christi means the tears of Christ, doesn't it?"

-"So let's raise our glasses to history, to Naples and to ourselves."

"I'm so happy to have you here, with me, il mio cuore.... These moments are engraved in my heart, forever..."

Maya, her eyes full of sweetness, had replied:

-"It's a journey I'll never forget...".

Perched high above the city, these terraces offered a breathtaking view of the Bay of Naples, with Vesuvius majestically dominating the horizon. The city lights sparkled before their eyes, creating an almost unreal atmosphere.

Maya had smiled, her eyes fixed on the landscape, but her mind seemed elsewhere.

-"It's true. Naples has a way of captivating you, of leaving a trace, you know? Like a story you never forget."

The two women had looked at each other for a moment, sharing a silent gaze filled with complicity and tenderness. Then Carina had broken the silence, evoking their adventure.

-"I hope we'll come back here one day..." she had said. "But for now, we've created our own story in Naples. And it's beautiful."

Maya had nodded, touched by her girlfriend's words.

-"I hope so too. But even if we don't come back, these three days will stay in my heart. This town, this view, but most of all... you."

- "You too..." the Sicilian had replied, gently taking her companion's hand under the table.

Their hands entwined, they had raised their glasses, not only to Naples, but to all they had just shared together.

"It's a goodbye, but... never an end..." had added Carina, savoring the sweet freshness of her cocktail.

And as night fell softly over the city, they sat there, enjoying every moment, knowing that this moment would remain etched in their memories well beyond their departure.

Thus ended their last day in Naples, taking with them not only precious memories but also the promise of an adventure yet to come, a story that was only just beginning.
