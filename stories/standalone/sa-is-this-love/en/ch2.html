-"If I had to find a reason to climb this tower again, it would be to deserve this meal..." Maya had replied with a dazzling smile.

The Italian looked offended.

-"I thought my kiss had convinced you to climb all the towers of Italy for me!"

- "all the towers of Italy to get such a kiss... And down them to taste the sumptuous dishes of your country..."

- "you can only have one reward!"

- "then I choose you." Replied the blonde, her eyes disconcertingly sincere, unnerving Carina.

- "allora... You'll get all the kisses you want from all the highest points in Italy and Sicily..."

After lunch, they set off for their next stop, a city still unknown to Maya, but which Carina promised would be fascinating. Seated on the passenger side, Maya consulted the photos they had taken so far, distractedly observing the landscape as it flashed by through the window.

-"You know..." began Maya, breaking the comfortable silence, "I always thought that cultural visits would be more of a chore than a pleasure; although yes, I really wanted to get to know Italy... But with you, it's... Different. I feel relaxed. Almost as if I could leave everything behind and concentrate on all this beauty... As if I'd never been that girl who'd devoted her life to sport..."

Carina had turned her head towards her, a gentle smile lighting up her face.

-"Do you think it's because you're on vacation?"

Maya had shaken her head.

-"No, it's not that. It's just you. You make it all... Easy. Natural."

A complicit silence had fallen, filled by the hum of the car. Carina had reached out to adjust the air-conditioning slightly before replying:

-"It's probably because I'm such a good guide. But be careful, I might make you climb some more towers as punishment for flattering me so much."

Maya had laughed, a frank and sincere sound.

-"For another kiss like the one this morning, I'm ready for anything."

The rest of the journey had been punctuated by little anecdotes told by Carina, who enjoyed sharing her love of Italy, between local legends and travel tips. With every kilometer, their complicity seemed to deepen, as if the road itself was weaving invisible links between them.
