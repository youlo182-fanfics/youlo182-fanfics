Twenty-year-old American Maya Bishop had always aimed high, but when she crossed the finish line first in the ten-thousand-meter race at the Paris Olympics, the magnitude of her success appeared to her in a new, almost overwhelming way. The crowd had cheered her on, but she could only think of all the hours she had spent running alone in the sun, rain, snow, extreme temperatures; sick, healthy, tired or not; the sweat and tears that had brought her to shine today.

After this victory, she had decided to take a step back.

-_"One year to savor" she murmured, almost as a promise to herself.

After this victory, the offers kept coming in: photo shoots for magazines, invitations from companies. She was even contacted by a famous cereal brand, becoming for a time the image of the breakfast of champions.

However, behind the glare of the cameras, Maya longed for something more concrete, more rooted in reality. And as autumn approached, a new adventure beckoned, this time far more arduous and committed: becoming a firefighter in Seattle. She had eagerly awaited the opening of applications and, as early as October, had applied.

The applications had finally opened, and Maya felt a surge of adrenaline, quite different from that of the competitions. This time, it wasn't about winning a medal, but proving she had what it took to face fire, fear and the unknown. She had to prepare a complete file, including a letter of motivation, in which she set out her desire to serve.

In this letter, she had tried to convey everything that was bubbling up inside her: discipline, resilience, and this thirst to give new meaning to her life.

« I know what it means to surpass oneself, but I want my courage to serve others » she wrote, conscious of the impact of her words. She explained that, despite the honors of her sporting career, she now aspired to a profession where solidarity and dedication took precedence over individual success.

She had also spent hours preparing for the written and physical tests. Each step would bring her closer to this new universe, propelling her beyond the comfort of stadiums and podiums.

Before receiving her final selection, Maya had to face several tests. Although her stamina and athletic discipline had prepared her for the challenge, these tests were designed to test candidates' ability to handle life-or-death situations.

The written tests included logical reasoning questions, emergency problem-solving scenarios and an assessment of her ability to communicate effectively under pressure. She had also studied principles of first aid and rescue techniques, in order to accurately answer questions on the basic procedures a firefighter must master. She had spent nights poring over training manuals, making sure nothing was left to chance. She was like that, once she had a goal in mind, she'd do anything to see it through to the end.

Then there were the physical tests, which represented the real challenge. Even for her, an Olympic athlete, each exercise demanded brute strength that she hadn't often had the opportunity to work on. She had to pass the « stair climb » test while carrying heavy equipment, simulate carrying a victim over several meters, or wield specialized tools to break down obstacles. She felt every muscle contract, every breath remind her of the harshness of the job to which she aspired. She knew she was cut out for such pressure, having proved it from an early age with running and more recently by winning her Olympic medal with a sprained ankle.

Each physical challenge brought her a little closer to her goal, but also plunged her into a new humility. As a runner, she'd never had to handle massive weights or struggle against the resistance of equipment, but this experience gave her a glimpse of the intense physical commitment this job demanded, and she wasn't afraid.

In March, after weeks of waiting, Maya had finally received an answer: she had been selected to join the fire academy in August 2025.

Ever since she was a little girl, Maya had thought of nothing but racing. She had put her heart and soul into athletics, never allowing herself other distractions. While her friends spent their money on outings, trips or fashionable gadgets, she had never felt the need to spend a penny on « frivolous » activities. Instead, she had saved every dollar, building her discipline and stamina with the patience of those who know their moment will come.

Thanks to her sponsors and the gold medal from the Paris Games, the income had come naturally. She had become the face of a famous cereal brand and a familiar face in magazines. Even so, her father, who had always watched her devote herself to her career, was worried about her spending some of her hard-earned fortune on a trip to Italy.

« Don't waste it on fleeting pleasures, Maya » he had told her. But she was convinced: after all those years of discipline and hard work, she'd earned this getaway. He'd advised her to invest in the stock market or put the money into a home, but before officially becoming a responsible adult, she wanted to indulge herself, just once be an irresponsible young girl, think only of herself and have a wild adventure.

So she had booked a private guide for a personalized tour of Italy, dropping a small fortune on a trip that she was sure would be worth every moment. As June approached, she couldn't wait to get out of the spotlight and into a more intimate experience, away from the stadiums and camera lenses, discovering Italian landscapes and flavors that would remain etched in her mind.

-"Are you coming to a photo with me?" she asked. Maya had nodded and approached, taking Carina's hand in a spontaneous gesture. A suspended moment. They looked at each other, the moment frozen in perfect complicity.

The next morning, Carina and Maya awoke to the calm of the mountains, ready to explore another of the Dolomites' natural treasures: Lake Braies. The crystal-clear waters of this alpine lake were surrounded by majestic, snow-capped mountains, creating a spectacle of serene beauty. They strolled quietly around the lake, the reflection of the trees and mountains in the water creating an almost magical atmosphere. The admiring blonde paused to savor the moment.

-"I think I could stay here all day... You know, I must confess that back home in the States, camping in national parks is a bit of a passion of mine... But I can see that Italy has nothing to be ashamed of when compared with the United States... These places are magnificent... I really wasn't expecting this..." she murmured, her gaze lost in the infinite.

Carina had smiled and approached her, stopping to place her hand on her shoulder.

- "This is the perfect place to disconnect, isn't it?"

The blonde had turned her head towards her and their eyes had met.

- "with the best partner possible..."

They had continued their walk, before sitting down on a bench facing the lake to enjoy the absolute silence, broken only by the gentle lapping of the water. A moment of pure tranquility, strengthening their bond even further.

The next morning, Carina had a surprise in store for Maya. As soon as they were ready, she drove her, without revealing their destination, to a fire station nestled in the heart of the Dolomites. Maya, intrigued, had arched an eyebrow when she saw the building appear at the bend in a winding pine-lined road.

"A barracks?" she had asked, her tone oscillating between curiosity and incredulity.

Carina had flashed a mischievous smile. "I thought you might be interested. Here, firefighters operate in the mountains, on unpredictable terrain. They have to be as agile as mountaineers and able to cope with extreme conditions. A little taste of what might be waiting for you one day."

Intrigued, Maya had followed Carina inside, where they had been greeted by a local firefighter, Pietro, a man with an imposing build and a warm smile. He had given them a tour of the fire station, showing them the specific equipment used for mountain rescues, such as stretchers adapted to cliffs and specialized ropes for abseiling.

Maya had approached one of the trucks, observing the sturdy chains attached to the tires. "It's fascinating," she'd murmured, a spark of admiration in her eyes. "They must be ready for anything here."

Carina had watched her, a tender smile on her lips. "I knew you'd like it."

Pietro, noting Maya's interest, had suggested she try out a climbing harness, used on perilous missions. Maya, always up for a challenge, accepted. As she adjusted the straps, Pietro explained the techniques of rescue in steep terrain, leaving Maya enthralled.

Carina, amused, had commented lightly: "Don't make too much of yourself, champion. They might want to recruit you before you've even started your academy."

Maya had laughed softly, her eyes shining with enthusiasm. "I'm impressed with everything they're doing here. It's a whole other level."

After thanking Pietro for his welcome, they had emerged, the still-shy sun illuminating the snowy peaks. Maya had given Carina a grateful look. "Thank you for the surprise. It motivates me even more for what lies ahead."

They had then enjoyed a quiet day in the picturesque Alpine village of Cortina d'Ampezzo, surrounded by majestic mountains. Strolling through the narrow streets lined with wooden chalets, admiring the small local stores. Carina, always curious about the region's traditions, had explained to Maya the history of the local artisans, their handmade products and local specialties. The picturesque setting and their complicity had made this day unforgettable.

-"Look at these carved wooden knives, they've been made by village craftsmen for generations!" explained Carina, pointing to a display case filled with handcrafted pieces.

Maya had smiled, fascinated by the authenticity of the place.

-"You really know all the good places, don't you?" she replied.

-"You've paid for a certain standard... I'm not only going to take you to touristy places to buy the same cheesy t-shirt that everyone else brings back, and make you eat at McDonald's or touristy restaurants to pay twenty euros for a pizza when you can eat a much better one for four..."

This reaction made the blonde smile. The Italian wasn't wrong, but that didn't make her any less adorable.

They had stopped off at a small local grocery store, where Carina had invited Maya to sample some of the local produce. The local cheese, speck and homemade jams awakened the taste buds of both young women.

After exploring the stores, they took a moment to rest on a bench facing the mountains, gazing up at the snow-capped peaks. The scene looked like something out of a painting, bathed in the soft light of the afternoon sun.

-"You know, it's for moments like these that I've wanted to travel... To visit the most beautiful country in the world... With the best food in the world..." said Maya, her eyes lost in the beauty of the landscape. "It's quiet, restful... perfect for really disconnecting."

- "And you don't even have to go to the other side of the world to feel elsewhere... At least for me..." replied Carina with a smile. "This is the heart of the Dolomites."

Their afternoon had continued with dinner at a local restaurant. The place, authentic but discreet, served refined Alpine cuisine. Carina, always full of surprises, had suggested that Maya try a traditional dish she was particularly fond of, canederli, typical Tyrolean bread dumplings served with a creamy cheese sauce.

"If you haven't tasted that here, you really haven't seen the essence of Alpine cuisine and that I've done my job badly!" She laughed.

Maya, amused, had agreed wholeheartedly and was savoring every bite.

-"It's delicious! How is it possible that I've lived so long without knowing this?"

Carina had looked at her tenderly, proud to be able to share this moment with her.

-"We still have so much to discover, believe me. But here in Cortina, everything seems a little more magical."

-"With you, I feel I could discover the world..." replied the blonde, gazing into her eyes.
