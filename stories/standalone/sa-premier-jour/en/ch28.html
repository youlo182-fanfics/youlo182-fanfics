-"ti amo il mio bambino... Tantissimo" replied Carina, running her hand against the back of his neck and bringing her son close to her body to embrace him, kissing his dark blond hair, while Maya had passed her second arm against their son's back too.

- "per tremila?" [times three thousand?] he asked.

Lucia had arrived in the kitchen at the same time, had seen the improvised hug and had come to put her arms against Liam's lower back and the captain's hip, who had then put her hand against her back as well.

-"molto di più. Tremila non bastano nemmeno per dire quanto vi amo tutti e tre." [much more than that. Three thousand isn't even enough to say how much I love all three of you].
