At eight o'clock, they'd been called into action. Ben had started back towards the hangar and Maya had passed him.

-"To the front desk, Warren!"

They'd gone into action, and they could all sense that the blonde was tense about being there. Not in relation to the fact of what had happened in the building earlier, but because she was here rather than at home. She was going to string together her day, night and next day's shift with Team D, and that wasn't on the agenda. They could imagine that she had hoped for something else on her return.

The intervention had gone much better without Ben, and although it had been complicated, Maya was more relaxed. She'd been in the thick of things and had been able to let off steam.

They had returned to the barracks after midnight and gone to their respective rooms or dormitories, trying to get some sleep.
Maya had gone in the direction of her office and had seen that her wife must have passed by because near the door of her bunker was a bag. She'd probably had to bring her some clothes, as she'd be going on duty the next day.

She retrieved her bag and returned to her captain's room.
She was about to switch on the light when she saw a shadow. She stepped back from the door, to let in a trickle of light, and saw her wife and their son in bed.

She smiled. It had even brought tears to her eyes. She had undone her shirt and come to lie against her wife's back, wrapping her arm around her body. She had rested her chin on her trapeze and her hand on their son, who was between the Italian and the wall.
Carina had turned her head slightly and had come to caress her wife's nose with her cheek.

-"I really needed this... I love you times three thousand", Maya whispered in her wife's ear before they both fell asleep.
