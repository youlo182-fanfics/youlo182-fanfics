The two Italians were whispering in their native tongue.

The blonde had returned and sat a little further away from Carina, still next to her, but further back on the sofa.

The firefighter had excused herself, claiming she had work to do and couldn't spend time with Carina.

"Don't worry, Gabri's waiting for me" she'd immediately regretted her sentence, and even more so when she'd seen the blonde clench her jaws.

She would be forever haunted by the women of her past, if anything were to happen between them, and she wasn't prepared to face that. They were friends and that was enough. Not to mention that, for the time being, she had to devote herself to the person who had brought her here and who was now growing in her womb.
