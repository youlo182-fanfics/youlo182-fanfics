They'd moved in two years ago when the blonde had received her tenure as a firefighter. They had a small house in Rome with three bedrooms and a small garden.

They were all watching television, waiting for the results of the presidential election.

- "I risultati sono arrivati!" [Go, go the results!] Said Ryan, who had moved back to Italy from Paris a few years ago. He had moved to Rome a year ago and had become very close to both women.

- "Arriverà, non essere così impaziente. Sono le otto e sono solo le cinquantotto." [It'll come, don't be impatient like that. It's eight o'clock and it's only fifty-eight] replied Maya.

The last minute had been the longest, and then the result fell on the TV screen.

Angelo Bonelli had beaten Sergio Mattarella by fifty-one point seventy-two percent of the vote.

They had all jumped for joy: the Verdi Sinistra party had won the Italian presidential elections. A complete turnaround for a country too long dominated by fascist parties.

Angelo Bonelli's party had tackled issues such as marriage for all, children of same-sex couples and other benefits for LGBTQIA+ people, a novelty in Italy.

Carina hugged her brother before turning to Maya and kissing her, holding her face with both hands.

The blonde had given a huge smile and when they had stepped back, she had bent down and put one knee on the ground while looking at Carina.

"Ora che è finalmente possibile. Vuoi rendermi la persona più felice del mondo diventando mia moglie??" [Now that it's finally possible. Will you make me the happiest person in the world by becoming my wife?]
