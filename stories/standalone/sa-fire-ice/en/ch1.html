The Venetian blinds were not closed and Carina had been able to see the blonde in that position with her back to the door.

Maya was so stiff, in her posture, her gestures, her words. And all of her firefighters were there at attention.
Carina had never seen such a strict captain. In fact, she had never had a female captain, she had always been in stations run by men.
What could have happened to this young woman to make her so strict? Was that why she was in the position she was in, when she looked like she was barely thirty years old? Or was it her way of getting respect, of protecting herself in a man's world? And what was that lost look she had after grabbing her arm? And her way of apologizing afterwards by stammering and going to lock herself in her office?
