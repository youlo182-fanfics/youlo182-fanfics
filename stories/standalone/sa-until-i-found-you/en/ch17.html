Maya was sitting on a hospital chair in a corridor. Her eyes stared into space. She hardly blinked, and her eyes continued to cry even without her consent.

She'd been in this hallway for just over an hour and yet it had felt so much longer, as if she'd been here for days, weeks, months.

Over and over again, she heard the sound of the helmet hitting the Hubba's concrete and Josephine screaming. Over and over again, she saw her girlfriend's body fall to the ground.

It was her fault. If Carina skateboarded, it was her fault. If Carina got upset, it was her fault. If Carina had been unconscious, if she had fallen, if she had been hurt, it was her fault.

She could feel her heart beating in her chest. She could feel it throughout her body. Every cell in her body was pounding.

Carina had been the one to make her heart beat. Carina and that very last second, the one before she went for a competitive run. And then it was Carina again. But it wasn't the fact that she could see her, feel her, hear her, it was the fact that she couldn't see her, feel her, hear her, and that she might never be able to again.

She wanted to try and think of the first time she'd seen her. That moment when Carina was still safe and sound, far from her, far from knowing her. Alive and well. Beautiful, looking at her from a distance and not yet knowing the sound of her voice.

She had lowered her eyes to her trembling hands and she could see that she had blood on her hands, why did she have blood on her hands? Whose blood was it? It couldn't be hers-she wasn't hurting anywhere.

She tried to search her mind. Firefighters. Firefighters had come for Carina. They'd dared to take her away from Carina.

Her elbow. Carina hadn't protected her elbow. She could see her hand passing over her arm, over her elbow, as the firefighters detached her from the body of her sweetheart. Robbing her of her very being.

That's when she found herself with blood in the palm of her hand. Of course, it could have been from her chin, but she hadn't touched it to avoid getting dirt on her wound.

She saw herself sitting on the floor, held by Jack, screaming.

«can she come with you?»

They'd refused to let her go upstairs with her girlfriend, so Jack held her in his arms, telling her that everything was going to be okay. That she'd left with the firemen.

«Get off me!» that was her voice.

How had she managed to speak? She felt as if she'd forgotten how. What was the brain's mechanism for speaking?

Jack had let go of Maya when the firefighters had left, and she could see herself getting up and grabbing Carina's skateboard. The skate she'd given to her girlfriend, putting it on the edge of the wall and punching it repeatedly. She remembered hearing Addison say she was going to take Taryn and Jo home and drop off their things at the blonde's house.

She knew it, that it was all her fault, that she hadn't kept her promise and that if it were possible she'd be the one lying on the cold operating table right now, not Carina. Not the most beautiful woman in the world. Not the love of her life. Not the person who made her heart beat.
