Leaving San Diego had a strange taste. Like closing a too-sweet parenthesis. For they knew that soon they would no longer be alone, and those they had been here were not yet ready to exist in front of others.

How could they hide who they had naturally become?

The road to Los Angeles was unhurried. Windows open. Low music. Bodies still soaked with salt and sun.

Carina watched the landscape unfold, her head resting against the window. Maya drove. Concentrated. But aware of every breath beside her.
