Carina was already there. Sitting on the sofa. She'd stood up almost immediately when she saw her, her gaze locked hers his with an attention she no longer even tried to hide.

- "Amore..."

Her voice had been gentle. But there was something behind it.

An expectation. A tension she couldn't fully control.

"You're home late..."

Maya had nodded slightly, without answering right away. She'd put her things down without really looking where, then stepped into the room.

Carina watched her. Every gesture. Every silence.

"Were you at the station? Or... at the hospital? At the academy, maybe? Or with your mother?" she asked, trying to keep her voice neutral.

Maya had stopped a few steps away from her.

- "Can we sit down?"

Carina's heart had clenched immediately. Not violently. But enough for her to feel it.

- "Bambina..."

A slight smile had come to die on the blonde's lips. Not exactly reassuring.

Not really sad either.

- "Please."

So Carina hadn't insisted. She'd sat down, slowly. As if each movement had taken on a little more weight.

Maya had followed her, taking her hand in hers without even thinking about it, her fingers coming to cling to his like a reflex.

Silence settled in. Not long, but already too long for Carina.

- "You'll kill me if you say nothing..."

Her voice had trembled slightly in spite of herself.

"You saw Dr. King... didn't you? What did she say?"

Maya had given a knowing smile, but hadn't answered. Not yet - and there was nothing foreboding about that smile. It was neither good nor bad. It just said «yes». In other words, «yes, I've seen Dr. King».

Her eyes had locked onto the Italian's, and there had been something in her gaze. Something Carina couldn't read completely.

Then her eyes had begun to shine.

And that... That had been enough.

Carina's breath caught. Her fingers had tightened around hers.

- "Bambina..."

Raw fear. Instinctive. Almost immediate.

"Dimmi" [Tell me...]

Her voice had broken.

"Ti prego, dimmi..." [I beg you, tell me...]

Maya had swallowed. Her fingers had tightened slightly around Carina's. Her lips had parted.

Her lips had parted. Then closed again. As if the words were hard to get out. As if she had to choose them carefully. As if telling her lover would make the situation real.

Carina hadn't moved. She didn't even dare to breathe properly.

- "Baby bear..." she said in a whisper, almost a prayer. The tears were already there, hanging from her eyelashes.

Maya had taken a breath. Just one breath. Her eyes never leaving hers.

And then... She had spoken.

- "Will you marry me?"
