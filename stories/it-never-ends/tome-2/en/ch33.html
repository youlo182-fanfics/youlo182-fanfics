Carina approached the railing, inhaling deeply. The night air carried a scent of dry grass and freedom.

The softness of the breeze, the warmth of his body against hers, the distant melody of the other visitors' voices. Everything was simple. Without pressure.

And yet, there was still that shadow inside her. That specter clinging to her thoughts, whispering that something was missing.

But tonight, she didn't want to think about it. She wanted to try. Try to let herself be carried away.

They sat on a rock, their breaths gradually coming easier. Jackson had pulled a bottle of flavored water from his bag and handed it to Carina before taking a sip.

The sun had begun to sink over the horizon as they sat on the Malibu pier.

The sky was ablaze with warm hues, shifting from pink to gold, from red to purple. The moment was suspended, almost unreal.

The Italian had let herself go against Jackson, her head resting on his shoulder.

- "It's beautiful..." she murmured.

- "yes..." he replied, placing a kiss on her hair.

But as her heart beat peacefully, a furtive thought had crossed her mind. Maya would have loved this moment. She'd closed her eyes briefly and inhaled deeply.

They had arrived in the early afternoon, greeted by the iodized scent of the ocean and the picturesque architecture of Santa Barbara.

The Spanish-inspired buildings gave the town a unique charm, and the Sicilian found herself enjoying the peaceful atmosphere.
