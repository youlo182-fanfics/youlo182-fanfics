Maya and Carina had decided to go skiing as a duo, just the two of them. No group, no noisy boys, no Jack, no Vic and especially no Jackson.

The sun beat down on the glistening snow as Carina struggled to keep up with Maya.

- "I'm supposed to be improving after a week, right?" she grumbled as she tried to steady her skis.

- "You have improved, babe, I promise!" Maya had turned to face her, flashing her a teasing smile.

- "Then why are you looking at me like I'm a walking disaster?"

- "Because you're the sexiest disaster I've ever seen on skis."

Carina rolled her eyes, but a smile betrayed her pride.

At one point, the blonde had wanted to teach her one last technique, a pro trick. Except that Carina had stiffened.

- "Enough, Maya! I just want to get down alive, not learn a pirouette for the Olympics!"

Maya burst out laughing.

- "It's not a pirouette, it's just a technique to turn better!"

- "Do you want me to turn better or survive?"

- "Both!"

But the brunette had raised her arms in defeat.

- "You know what? I give up. I'm going to sulk on the chairlift."

- "You could probably win a medal for sulking!"

- "Yeah, well so could you, Maya Bishop! Who do you think spent a week sulking about this and that?!"

And she had turned on her heel, leaving Maya laughing as she joined her. The sprinter hadn't taken it badly; she knew it was the truth. But she knew it, she understood it. Between her and Carina it was mutual and of the same intensity.

In the chairlift, the Sicilian had crossed her arms, falsely offended.

The blonde had turned to her, a smile on the corner of her lips.

- "Are you really giving your baby Bear a hard time?"

Carina had raised an eyebrow, without answering, so Maya had blown on her neck. The brunette had immediately flinched.

- "Maya! Stop it!"

- "Oh? It's annoying, isn't it?"

- "I'm going to kill you."

The blonde had continued, teasingly, until Carina could resist no longer and grabbed her by the jacket, pulling her close for a quick but fiery kiss.

The sprinter had smiled against her lips.

- "I love it when you're frustrated."

Carina had pinched her thigh.

- "And you're not afraid of me kissing you in public?!"

- "I'd say I was teasing you about New Year's..."

- "Your father would love that... He would love to know that it's not the first time we've kissed..."

- "I think you can say whatever you want... After this morning, nothing will be able to make me angry all day..."

Carina had bit her lip and blushed.

- "This morning it was..." Her smile had broadened and she had blushed even more.

Maya had looked at her and she too had started to blush.

- "It was perfect..."

- "Perfect..."

The city sparkled in the afternoon sun, lively but peaceful.

Hand in hand, on the pretext that they were cold and that Carina had lost her gloves, so they had each taken one from Maya, they had stopped in front of a small souvenir shop.

The blonde had picked up a snowflake-shaped key ring and twirled it between her fingers.

- "It may be a cliché, but... I want us to bring something back."

Carina had smiled and approached.

- "Shall we take the same one?"

Maya had looked at her, before whispering:

- "Yeah. That way, we'll always have a piece of this trip with us..."

Carina had nodded, then casually said:

- "One day, we should take a trip to Italy, just the two of us."

Maya had looked up at her.

For a moment, she said nothing. She just looked at Carina, at her seriousness, at her hope concealed beneath a joke.

Then she smiled.

- "One day, we will go."

It wasn't a joke. Not an empty promise.

One day, they would go.

The atmosphere on the bus was different from the way it had been on the way there. Less excitement, more tiredness.

Maya and Carina were sitting side by side, under a blanket.

The sound of the engine, the gentle rocking of the vehicle... everything was conducive to sleep.

But the blonde wasn't sleeping yet. The brunette was. Her head was resting on the sprinter's shoulder, her fingers intertwined with hers under the blanket.

Maya had turned her head slightly, observing her sleeping face, then, without thinking, she had planted a kiss on her forehead, taking advantage of the fact that the bus was plunged into darkness. And, in an almost imperceptible whisper, she had let the words slip, in Italian.

"Buona notte, amore mio..."

She hadn't realized that Carina wasn't completely asleep.

That she had heard. And that she had smiled against her shoulder, eyes still closed.
