/Flashback/

It was a little after half past eight when Maya's phone rang.

It was her brother, Mason. Why was Mason calling her at eight o'clock on a Sunday morning? She picked up the phone immediately.

/Phone conversation/

- "Mase?"

- "Maya..."

- "yes?"

- "uh..." he began hesitantly.

- "Mason are you in custody?"

- "what?! No !"

- "Is it mom? ... Dad?"

- "n-n-no..."

The blonde had swallowed. She sensed something was wrong.

- "Mase... You worry me..."

- "it's... It's her."

She hadn't even needed to ask who Mason was talking about.

She'd felt her heart stop for a second. She'd sat up in slow motion. Stretching her arm toward her bed, to keep from falling to the floor.

- "H-h-her?"

Mase had heard his sister's voice, trembling, tears in her voice. He'd even been able to hear that her heart had stopped.

- "oh no! P-p-pardon! No, she's fine!"

Maya had released a breath she didn't know she was holding, trembling. She had placed her hand on her heart, and blinking, her tears had flowed, while her breathing was rapid.

"Excuse me..." Mason added after a few long seconds.

- "w-why are you... Are you calling me? Just to see if I still cared about her?!" She asked irritably.

- "Maya, her... Her father got shot."

- "W-What?"

- "last night... Last night I was with Andrea... We were together when he got a call from his mother..."

- "I don't understand why you're calling me... what do you want me to do?"

- "Carina's coming."

- "and?"

- "Maya! Don't be so stupid! The girl you love is coming. She's coming because her father's in the hospital."

- "I don't love her!"

- "Yeah, try to convince yourself of that... She might need your support."

- "she'll probably come with her boyfriend."

- "Maya... Don't be stupid! You wouldn't have reacted the way you did if she didn't mean anything to you."

- "what do you want me to do?!"

- "call her. Go to the hospital, I don't know, but don't stay there, at home. Show her, that despite everything, you're there for her."

- "... No, I... I don't think so."

- "and... If her father dies?"

- "why are you trying to make me feel guilty?!"

- "I'm trying to get you to react... If the same thing happened to our father, wouldn't you want her to come?"

- "She wouldn't."

- "well yeah, that's why she asks Andrea about you every time she gets him on the phone."

Maya had frowned but hadn't answered.

"Wait... Please don't tell her I told you."

- "I..." She'd paused, "I'm not talking to her. I can't tell her."

- "Maya..." He made as if to tell her that she despaired of her.

- "what... What happened?"

- "I only have details, but... It seems he was shot by a... By the husband of a patient he... That he killed in surgery..."

- "and his... His life is... Well, he... He'll... Survive?"

- "I don't know... Wait, I... I have a message from Andrew... He... Tells me Carina's landing around nine."

- "okay."

There had been a blank, then Mase had spoken again:

- "he's at Seattle Pres... If you change your mind."

- "okay."

They finally hung up.

/End of phone conversation/

At more than ten o'clock, Andy had burst into Maya's room.

- "Did you hear?!" She shouted as she came storming in.

- "Heard what? Your guy sneaking in at seven in the morning with his big elephant steps?"

- "No. Carina's dad."

- "oh..."

- "oh...? So yes?!" She asked almost annoyed at her best friend.

- "yes. Mase called."

- "and?!"

- "And what?" Asked the blonde, casually.

- "are you serious?!"

- "what?"

- "Wait, are you kidding me? You and Carina are, like, so close to each other and you're not going to the hospital?"

- "We were. Have you felt like we've been in the same room for two years?"

- "yeah, well, you can't help it if circumstances meant that the times she was able to come back, you had to go!"

- "yeah... Of course... That's it..." Maya replied as if to say « that's it ». She wondered how her best friend and all their friends could be so blind.

- "I'm not stupid. I'm well aware that since she's been dating Jackson, there's something broken between you.... But it's still Carina. Your Carina."

- "It's not... « my Carina »." Replied the blonde, clenching her fist and jaws.

- "us, we're going to the hospital. And I think if anything happens... Carina will be happy... Despite the circumstances, that you're here."

- "I... I don't think so. Besides, Mase texted me earlier and he said her boyfriend was there. He's the one she needs. Not me." Replied the sprinter, averting her gaze.

- "I know you never wanted to tell me... But if you'd fallen in love with Carina, I'd understand. She's gorgeous and above all... She used to hug you all the time in high school. I'd understand if you'd developed feelings for her... And her dating Jackson broke something between you... In you.

But it's been over two years. And yes, she has a new boyfriend... But she's also the girl who keeps asking me if it's true that you're okay... As if she didn't believe your messages..."

Maya had twitched her eyebrows. If they all knew that they no longer wrote to each other privately, apart from « happy new year » or « happy birthday ».

"Maya?"

- "I'll... I'll think about it."

- "Yes, well... Don't think too much Jo, Ryan, Jack and I are going to the hospital.... We want to be there for our friend."

/End of flashback/

Carina's body in Maya's arms had begun to tremble and the blonde had then run her hand down the Italian's back.

- "I'm here Car'."

The moment had lasted an eternity for the rest of their friends.

The Sicilian had finally stepped back and the blonde had passed her but over Carina's cheeks.

"I'm sorry about your father..."

The Italian was about to say something but she'd felt another hand pass over her back and she'd immediately felt her heart clench.

Obviously, she hadn't come alone, and her reunion with Maya had to be spoiled by the presence of her boyfriend. Her boyfriend whom she now had to introduce to Maya.

- "I... This is Winston." The brunette said, backing away from Maya's embrace.

They had all shaken hands with her and at the same time hugged Lucia and Andrea.

- "do you all want to come home?" Lucia asked.

- "Maybe we'll leave you with your family..." Andy replied. Not wanting to intrude on their grief.

Carina had slipped her hand into Maya's and Lucia had seen her gesture.

- "I think my children need their friends... And... And it'll take my mind off things."

The DeLuca house was immersed in a hushed atmosphere of fatigue and sadness. Lucia was busy in the kitchen, reheating a dish of lasagne prepared the day before, while Andrea set the table with an absent air, Mason helping him with the task. The burden of the last few hours weighed heavily on their shoulders.

In the living room, conversations remained low, almost whispered, as if everyone feared disturbing the fragile balance of the moment. Carina sat between Maya and Winston on the sofa, her body stretched between these two opposing presences. She knew Winston could feel it. He said nothing, but his gaze scrutinized her every move, as if trying to read something she didn't want to say aloud. He'd never heard of this Maya, at least not like the Italian's other friends, and yet it was in her arms that she'd thrown herself into the hospital while he was there.

Maya, for her part, had remained strangely silent. Sitting with her elbows on her knees, she absent-mindedly played with her fingers, avoiding meeting Carina's gaze for too long. Andy and the others were trying to lighten the mood by chatting about small talk, but they all knew that this impromptu dinner was more an excuse to surround the DeLucas than a real moment of levity.

When Lucia announced that the meal was ready, everyone stood up and took their places around the table, Winston to Carina's left, Maya to her right. A shiver ran through the Italian as the blonde poured her a glass of water, briefly brushing her fingers. Winston hadn't missed either the gesture or the reaction.

The meal had been punctuated by silences. Lucia, despite her obvious pain, had tried to maintain composure by asking questions about each other's lives.

After the meal. They all remained around the table. Lucia, the perfect host, had made some good Italian coffee and brought back some small cakes.

They were all quiet, lost in thought, when suddenly Andrea burst out laughing. Shocking everyone.

- "Andrea!" Carina said.

He was laughing, but there were tears in his eyes.

- "sorry... Sorry... It's just that I've been thinking about the time we went to visit Etna. I mean, the time we went with Graziella... when... When we climbed up on that crater and, Dad's foot slipped on lava rocks and he got to the bottom of the crater, tobogganing on his butt and pretended it was on purpose, because he didn't want to be ashamed."

Everyone laughed. Even Carina, who had hardly spoken to her father in two and a half years. There was still that little girl inside her who had affection for her father. For the father he had once been.

Lucia, Andrea and Carina had then continued to tell far-fetched and funny anecdotes about Vincenzo, as if that would make his passing easier to accept or at least come to terms with.

At six thirty. The Italian leaned toward her boyfriend.

- "maybe you should look into flights to San Fran."

- "Are you sure you don't want me to stay?" he asked, turning to Carina and placing his hand on her arm.

She'd pursed her lips, aware of the stares on her. Maya's.

- "You've got class tomorrow... I can make excuses, but you can't..."

Winston had nodded slowly, as if waiting for something else, a sign, a request she wouldn't make. Finally, he'd grabbed his phone and found a plane leaving in just over an hour and a half.

- "I'll call a cab." He said after paying the fare.

No one had reacted immediately, sensing the slight tension, sensing that the Italian almost didn't want him to stay. Carina had lowered her head, her hands clasped around her towel. Maya, for her part, had stared at an invisible point in front of her.

A few minutes later, Winston had finally stood up, declaring that his cab was on its way. He'd said good-bye to everyone, and Carina had accompanied him to the front of the house, where a car was already waiting.

"If you ever... need anything, you call me, okay?"

Carina had nodded, but her smile was strained. Winston had detailed her for a moment, then glanced toward the half-open door, where Maya had passed going from the living room to the kitchen.

He'd sketched a sad smile.

"Take care."

Carina had nodded one last time, stretching out her face to kiss him on the lips. Then he'd pulled away, rushing into the cab, which had then disappeared into the night.

When she turned back towards the house, she felt her heart clench.

Maya was still there, just a few feet away. Silent. Attentive. She had just come out of the kitchen and they were face to face. No doubt she'd seen that kiss. No doubt she'd looked away the moment it happened.

And Carina knew she'd have to talk to her.

The brunette had swallowed before entering the house. She approached Maya, who was unable to move. Her heart was racing.

She raised her hand. As if she had to be sure that Maya was there, real.

She'd put her hand on her jaw and her thumb had brushed against her cheekbone in a suspended moment.

- "thank you for coming..."

- "it's... It's okay... If... If it had been the other way around... You'd have come too."

- "I would have come alone."

- "It was Mase and Andy's idea..."

- "no... Not that... If I knew you were coming... I would... I would have come alone..."

- "He's your boyfriend." Maya replied as if it were a foregone conclusion.

- "But it's been almost two years since we've seen each other..."

- "six hundred and ninety-four days..." Replied the blonde.

The brunette had approached her without waiting and taken her in her arms.

As they stood in the corridor between the front door and the kitchen door, the light had come on and they had all appeared.

- "we... We'll leave you now..." Jo said.

They stepped back from each other and took turns to hug Carina.

- "Send a message or call when you need Carina." Andy said, taking the brunette in her arms.

- "we're all here for you." Jack added in turn.

- "And especially, if any of you need anything. Even... go to grocery store for you, don't hesitate. I can stop by whenever there's a need." Ryan added. He then turned to Lucia and Andrea: "even when Carina goes back to San Francisco."

- "You have wonderful friends, my girl." Lucia said with emotion, taking Ryan's hand to thank him.

Maya had started to turn to Carina to say goodbye too, and the brunette had slipped her hand into hers.

- "Will you stay the night?... I don't... I don't really feel like being alone tonight and... And I haven't seen you in ages..."

The blonde had felt her heart quicken. It wasn't as if Carina had offered her « pancakes » right there in front of everyone. Nothing like that. She probably wanted to talk and, not be alone.

She'd hesitated, maybe a little too long, but she'd said it herself, six hundred and ninety-four days, far too long for a woman she thought about every day.

- "if Lucia agrees."

- "of course, Maya. You've always been welcome in this house."

Carina had looked up into her brother's eyes. Their mother had no idea how untrue that was. Ever since Vincenzo had caught Carina and Maya about to kiss on the third of May, two years before, she hadn't been welcome there, not by Lucia, not by Andrea, or by Carina, but by Vincenzo.

Their friends had eventually left, and the Italian had turned to the blonde.

- "Do you mind if we stay a little longer with my mother and brother before we go upstairs?"

- "No. Of course, Carina."

Lucia had gone to the kitchen to do a bit of housework and her daughter had come to see her.

- "Mamma, vedremo domani, mi fermerò per un po'. Lo farò da sola." [mamma, we'll see tomorrow, I'll stay a while. I'll do it myself].

- "ma cosa farò ora, senza tuo padre..." [but what am I going to do now, without your father...]

Carina had looked up at Maya, who was talking to Andrea.

- "impari a vivere senza la persona che ami..." [you'll learn to live without the person you love...]

The brunette hadn't wanted to dwell on the reason for her words. She put her arm around her mother's shoulders and led her to the sofa. As she passed, she took Maya's hand and Andrea followed. They sat down on the sofa, Lucia between her children and Carina between her mother and the blonde.

They turned on the TV and Lucia began to weep silently. Carina was heartbroken to see her mother so sad, and surprisingly, Maya didn't feel uncomfortable being in the middle of this family. Of course, the « mourning » aspect was delicate, but she really felt it was her place to be with Carina in this moment of grief.

Lucia was holding hands with her two children, and the blonde had started to look at Carina's profile. Even in mourning, she was the most beautiful woman in the world. Maya had slid her hand up the Italian's thigh as if to say, « I'm here », and the Sicilian had slipped her arm under hers.

The four of them stayed like that, until Lucia began to fall asleep, exhausted by the day, by her tears, by her loss.

Carina had tucked her mother in, as she used to do when she was little, and then gone to say good night to her brother and talk to him for a few moments.

Then she'd gone back to her room. A room she'd never really been in anymore, and the sight had taken her breath away. Maya was there, in front of her dressing table, looking at the photos hanging on the wall. The picture of the two of them.

She had swallowed, if only she could move, she'd come and slide her hands over the blonde's cheeks and she'd come and kiss her. But she was pinned down, unable to move.

Maya had finally turned around.

- "would you have pajamas for me, please?"

- "y-y-yeah."

They both froze and thought back to the last time the sprinter had been there. The day Carina had been forced to break her heart. The fifth of May, two thousand and twenty-four. As if they could forget that date.

In the end, the Sicilian had gone to her wardrobe to get a pair of shorts and a T-shirt for Maya, and they had taken turns getting dressed for bed.

When she returned to her room, the blonde was already on one side of the bed, typing on her phone.

- "Uh... I put myself on this side... Like... Like before."

- "no... No worries."

Carina had come to stand beside her and Maya had put her phone on the bedside table.

"I... I don't feel like... To talk tonight... You probably have class or practice tomorrow... But we could talk... later?"

- "No worries Carina. We don't even have to talk if you want. I'll understand."

- "Thanks Bambina."

The blonde had frozen, she hadn't heard that nickname in far too long...

They had finally gone to bed, and once the light had gone out, Carina had come to lie in the blonde's arms. She had slid her face against the American's throat and one of her hands had been placed on the opposite side of the sprinter's neck.
